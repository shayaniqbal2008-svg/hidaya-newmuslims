# Positioning: a curriculum built around converts' real pain points

> [!idea] Shayan's insight (2026-10-05)
> Existing new-Muslim guides teach general knowledge about the religion. Ours is built around the problems converts actually say they face. That makes it more useful to them, and it's our unique advantage and a pitching point.

## The claim
Most new-Muslim material answers *"What does Islam teach?"* Ours also answers *"What do I do about the thing that's hard for me this week?"*: telling my family, praying in a non-Muslim home, people treating me differently in hijab, feeling lonely after the shahada, and feeling my iman drop and fearing that means I'll leave.

## Evidence it holds up
- **The pain points:** [[Personal Research via reddit on pain points for converts]] (family is Reddit's #1 topic; loneliness; the honeymoon ending; iman dips) and [[Research Notes - Retention and Convert Voices]] (loneliness top theme; Yaqeen 2025: 40% lonely at Ramadan/Eid; 23% have considered leaving).
- **The gap in existing sources:** full-text search of *The Revealed Path* found zero mentions of non-Muslim parents, praying at home, hijab reactions, iman going up and down, or family holidays ([[Source - The Revealed Path]]). Across T1's 15 books, these are listed as "commonly missing" ([[Research Report]] §5).
- **Why it matters for retention:** the risk period after the shahada is social, not theological ([[Research Report]] §1, finding 3).

## Honest caveats (so the pitch doesn't overclaim)
- A few sources touch some of these: *The New Muslim's Field Guide* leads with family, hijab and culture; newmuslims.com covers "breaking the news to family" in Level 1; Rabata's Project Lina has "Tend Your Ties." So the claim is **not** "nobody covers this." It's that **no one combines pain-point-first content with a structured daily program and a masjid that sees who's struggling.**
- *The New Muslim's Field Guide* is the closest competitor in content: it covers family (with a letter to hand relatives), burnout, the first masjid visit and the few-weeks-in slump ([[Source - The New Muslims Field Guide]]). It's still a book read once, with no daily sequence, no step-by-step prayer and no masjid layer.
- The Reddit findings are a personal read of forum threads, not a survey. Link the threads before quoting them publicly.

## What it means for the build
- **T2:** every phase has pain-point lessons placed *when the problem usually hits* (family in week 1, the honeymoon ending around days 21–30, Ramadan by calendar), not in a "lifestyle" appendix at the end. Where each one lands: the coverage table in [[Outline - 90 Days]].
- **Product:** the masjid dashboard shows who has gone quiet, so a person reaches out at the moment the pain points predict.
- **Pitch line (draft):** *"Other programs teach converts about Islam. Ours walks them through the first 90 days as they actually happen: telling family, praying at home, the loneliness after the shahada, and the day their iman dips. And it tells the masjid who needs a call."*
