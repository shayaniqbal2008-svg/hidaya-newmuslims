## AI guide context
**Lesson facts**
- Most Muslims had already migrated to Madinah in small groups; Quraysh feared he would join them and plotted to restrain, kill or expel him [Q 8:30; IslamQA 11575].
- He had 'Ali sleep in his bed [IslamQA 11575; Dar al-Ifta Egypt (Hijrah)], and 'Ali stayed to return the valuables people had entrusted to him [IslamQA 11575] (tell this with "the early sources report").
- He came to Abu Bakr's house at midday, an unusual time, to say permission to leave had come; Abu Bakr asked to accompany him [Bukhari 3905].
- Asma' bint Abi Bakr tied the food bag with her waistband and was called Dhat al-Nitaqayn, "the owner of two belts" [Bukhari 3905].
- They hired a trusted non-Muslim guide (named 'Abdullah ibn Urayqit in IslamQA 11575), who led them by an unusual route along the coast [Bukhari 3905].
- The Companions later dated the Muslim calendar from the hijra [Bukhari 3934]; Day 79 covers this.

**Likely questions**
- *"Didn't he throw dust and walk right past the assassins?"* The early biographers relate it, but its chain wasn't checked for this track, so we leave it out. What's certain is Q 8:30.
- *"Why trust a guide who wasn't Muslim?"* He was skilled and trustworthy; they gave him their camels and he kept his word [Bukhari 3905]. Islam values competence and honesty in anyone, and converts can work closely with non-Muslim friends and family.
- *"What is tawakkul?"* Relying on Allah after doing your part. Planning isn't a lack of faith.
- *"How do I stay honest with people who mock me?"* Honesty doesn't depend on how others act; calm, kind, firm. If mockery turns into harassment at work, suggest documenting it and talking to HR and the mentor.

**Escalate:** nothing specific; workplace harassment goes to the mentor (and HR), not a ruling.

**Don't**
- Don't tell the dust story as fact.
- Don't add that Asma' was pregnant (unneeded detail).
- Don't use "even his enemies" to suggest the convert's family or coworkers are enemies.
