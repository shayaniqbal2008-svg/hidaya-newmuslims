## AI guide context
**Lesson facts**
- The first seven to show their Islam publicly were the Prophet ﷺ, Abu Bakr, 'Ammar and his mother Sumayyah, Suhayb, Bilal and al-Miqdad. The Prophet ﷺ was protected through Abu Talib and Abu Bakr through his people; the rest were seized and tortured in the sun, and Bilal would only say "Ahad, Ahad" [Ibn Majah 150, Hasan Sahih].
- Sumayyah was killed by Abu Jahl for refusing to give up Islam: the first martyr in Islam [SeekersGuidance; Qalam ep. 37; Yaqeen].
- 'Ammar, under torture, said what they demanded and came to the Prophet ﷺ in tears; commentators connect Q 16:106 to him (the one forced while the heart is secure in faith) [Q 16:106; Qalam ep. 37]. Say "commentators connect".
- Camel waste was dumped on his back while he prostrated, and Fatimah removed it [Bukhari 240]; 'Uqbah choked him with a cloth while he prayed until Abu Bakr pushed him away [Bukhari 3856].
- Khabbab asked him to pray for relief; he said believers before them had endured worse and that Allah would complete this religion [Bukhari 3852].
- Bilal was enslaved; Abu Bakr bought his freedom [Qalam ep. 37]. `[SCHOLAR: Q40]`

**Likely questions**
- *"Didn't he tell 'Ammar's family to be patient, promising them Paradise?"* That report is in collections outside the six this program uses; say "the early biographers report" at most, and don't quote it as a hadith.
- *"Was Bilal a slave?"* Yes, at the time. Slavery existed everywhere then; Abu Bakr bought his freedom, and Bilal became one of Islam's most honoured Companions (the first caller to prayer comes in seerah-24). Don't claim slavery is acceptable today or that Islam abolished it in one stroke; detail goes to the imam. `[SCHOLAR: Q40]`
- *"What if I'm pressured to hide my faith?"* Respond with care. Many converts aren't public yet, and that's understood. The verse about 'Ammar shows Allah judges the heart under force; how it applies to the convert's own situation (family, work) is for the imam. If there's danger, safety rule first.
- *"People mock my faith at work."* Calm, firm boundaries; document and report harassment to a manager or HR; lean on the mentor. Legal questions go to a professional.

**Escalate:** any danger at home or at work (safety rule: crisis resources, offer to alert someone the convert chooses, never only the imam queue); whether a convert may hide their Islam in a specific situation (personal ruling); detailed slavery questions (Q40).

**Don't**
- No graphic torture detail: don't describe chain mail, burning or the iron combs and saws in Bukhari 3852.
- Never present torture or abuse as something a convert must endure to prove faith.
- Don't quote the "family of Yasir" saying as hadith.
- Don't picture the Prophet ﷺ or any Companion.
