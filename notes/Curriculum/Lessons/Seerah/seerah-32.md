## AI guide context
**Lesson facts**
- In 8 AH he sent an army north toward Byzantine lands; it met a much larger force at Mu'tah [IslamQA 11575 ("the Romans gathered a huge army")]. IslamQA 305386 lists it among his campaigns though he didn't go himself. Single organisation: say "IslamQA reports" or "much larger". Don't give army sizes (3,000 and larger figures are sirah only).
- He appointed Zayd ibn Harithah, then Ja'far if Zayd was killed, then 'Abdullah ibn Rawahah if Ja'far was killed [Bukhari 4261].
- All three were killed. He told the people before the news arrived, with tears in his eyes, then said a sword from Allah's swords (Khalid) took the flag and Allah gave them victory [Bukhari 4262].
- Zayd: one of the first believers; a former slave he had freed and raised as a son [Qalam ep. 32; IslamQA 11575]. `[SCHOLAR: Q40]`
- Ja'far: his cousin; spoke for the Muslims before the Negus and recited from Surah Maryam [Qalam ep. 43; Dar al-Ifta Egypt, seerah part 9]; returned from Abyssinia after Khaybar (7 AH) [Dar al-Ifta Egypt, seerah part 9].
- His rules of war: no killing of women or children [Bukhari 3015]. `[SCHOLAR: Q39]`

**Likely questions**
- *"Did the Muslims win at Mu'tah?"* Historians describe the outcome differently. Use the hadith's words: Khalid took the flag and Allah gave them victory [Bukhari 4262]. Don't add numbers or battle detail.
- *"Who was Zayd? Was he a slave?"* Say plainly: slavery existed everywhere then; Zayd had been kidnapped as a child and sold; the Prophet ﷺ freed him and raised him as a son, and he became one of the first believers. Don't claim slavery is acceptable today; detailed questions go to the imam. (The later change to adoption rules is not taught here; route to the imam.) `[SCHOLAR: Q40]`
- *"Why fight the Byzantines?"* Keep it to history; IslamQA reports a large Roman army gathered. No rulings on fighting today (Day 71 frame). `[SCHOLAR: Q39]`
- *"I'm grieving. What does Islam say?"* Listen first. Tears are mercy [Bukhari 1303]; patience and "we belong to Allah and to Him we return" [Q 2:155–157]; Day 77 covers grief in full. If the person who died wasn't Muslim, follow Day 77's handling: comfort first, no comment on anyone's fate, offer the imam. `[SCHOLAR: Q23]`

**Escalate:** a convert's deceased non-Muslim relative (Q23); detailed questions on slavery or adoption (Q40); fighting today (imam); grief that sounds like depression or crisis (safety rule and mentor).

**Don't**
- Don't describe wounds or the fighting (Bukhari 4261's detail about Ja'far's wounds is not for screens or the AI guide's own initiative).
- Don't call tears weak faith or rush a grieving convert to "be patient".
- Don't comment on any non-Muslim relative's fate.
