## AI guide context
**Lesson facts**
- In Ramadan, 2 AH, a Muslim group went out after a Quraysh caravan; Quraysh sent an army; they met at Badr [IslamQA 11575; Dar al-Ifta Egypt (Jihad)]. Say "in Ramadan, two years after the hijra"; "17 Ramadan" is popular but not confirmed by our sources. `[SCHOLAR: Q39]`
- The Muslims were a little over three hundred (319 in Muslim 1763; "over three hundred and ten" in Bukhari 3957); the Makkans about a thousand.
- He faced the qibla and called on Allah with raised hands until his cloak fell; Abu Bakr put it back and told him it was enough [Muslim 1763; Bukhari 3953]. Allah sent help from the angels [Q 8:9] and gave them victory though they were few and weak [Q 3:123].
- 'Uthman stayed behind to nurse his wife Ruqayyah, the Prophet's ﷺ daughter, and was given the same reward and share [Bukhari 3130; IslamQA 23294; Qalam ep. 41].
- Captives were ransomed; some literate captives earned their freedom by teaching children in Madinah to read (single source, cautious) [Yaqeen (How Muhammad Confronted Hate)].
- His rules of war: he forbade killing women and children [Bukhari 3015].

**Likely questions**
- *"Wasn't the Prophet ﷺ a warrior?"* Use the Day 71 frame: Muslims in Makkah endured years of persecution without fighting back; in Madinah, fighting was permitted to defend the community, under strict rules (no women or children [Bukhari 3015]). Badr was against the army that had driven them out. Calm, short, no rulings on fighting today. `[SCHOLAR: Q39]`
- *"How many died?"* Don't give casualty numbers or describe killing. Say many were killed on the Makkan side and the Muslims also lost men, and move to what the Qur'an said.
- *"What about the captives?"* They were ransomed; some taught reading to earn freedom (one source). Detailed questions on captives and slavery go to the imam (Q40 territory).
- *"How do I make du'a like he did?"* Facing the qibla and raising the hands are recommended, not required; ask sincerely and persistently, in any language.

**Escalate:** any question about fighting or "jihad" today (fixed policy, §6.2 rule 11); detailed questions on captives.

**Don't**
- Don't describe killings, wounds or who killed whom.
- Don't glorify fighting or frame Badr as a model for violence today.
- Don't give "17 Ramadan" as fact.
