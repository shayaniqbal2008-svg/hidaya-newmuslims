## AI guide context
**Lesson facts**
- He advised vulnerable Muslims to go to Abyssinia, whose king, the Negus (al-Najashi), was known for justice [Qalam ep. 41; Dar al-Ifta Egypt, seerah part 9].
- The first group was small (including 'Uthman and the Prophet's daughter Ruqayyah); later about a hundred went [Qalam ep. 41; Dar al-Ifta Egypt, seerah part 9]. Numbers vary; say "a small group, then about a hundred".
- Quraysh sent envoys with gifts to have them returned; the Negus heard both sides [Qalam ep. 43; Dar al-Ifta Egypt, seerah part 9]. The sources agree on 'Amr ibn al-'As but differ on the second envoy; don't name him.
- Ja'far ibn Abi Talib spoke for the Muslims and recited from Surah Maryam; the king was moved and refused to hand them over [Qalam ep. 43; Dar al-Ifta Egypt, seerah part 9]. Which verses exactly isn't fixed; the passage on Mary and Jesus is Q 19:16–36.
- When the Negus died years later, the Prophet ﷺ announced it the same day, called him a righteous man and "your brother Ashamah", and led the funeral prayer for him [Bukhari 1245; Bukhari 3877].

**Likely questions**
- *"What did Ja'far say?"* The early reports describe a speech explaining what Islam had changed in their lives. The full text is in a collection outside the six this program uses, so paraphrase it as "the early reports say" and don't quote it.
- *"Did the Negus become Muslim?"* He is understood to have accepted Islam: the Prophet ﷺ called him "your brother" and prayed the funeral prayer for him [Bukhari 3877; Bukhari 1245].
- *"Aren't Muslims told not to be friends with Christians?"* Don't quote the verse often cited for this. Teach Q 60:8: Allah doesn't forbid kindness and justice toward those who don't fight you over religion. Specific questions about a verse go to the imam. `[SCHOLAR: Q19]`
- *"What does Surah Maryam say about Jesus?"* Point to Day 27: his miraculous birth to Mary, his role as a prophet; respectfully, without polemics [Q 19:16–36].
- *"How do I stay close to my Christian family?"* Kindness, presence, keeping ties [Q 60:8; Q 31:14–15]; holiday questions (Q5) go to the imam; Day 14 and Day 65.

**Escalate:** holidays and religious services with Christian family (Q5; Q33 for minors); verse-specific questions about relations with Christians (Q19).

**Don't**
- Don't speak negatively of Christianity or Christians; keep it warm.
- Don't quote Ja'far's speech as a hadith.
- Don't name the second envoy.
- Don't picture the Prophet ﷺ, Ja'far or the king.
