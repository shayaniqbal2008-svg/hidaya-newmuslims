## AI guide context
**Lesson facts**
- Quraysh's allies broke the Hudaybiyah truce [IslamQA 11575] (single text source; "IslamQA reports"). In Ramadan, 8 AH, eight and a half years after the hijra, he set out with ten thousand [Bukhari 4276].
- He announced that whoever entered Abu Sufyan's house would be safe [Muslim 1780]; Abu Sufyan, long an enemy, had just accepted Islam [Yaqeen, Confronting Hate].
- Some of the Ansar thought love of his city would keep him in Makkah; he told them he had migrated to Allah and to them, and would live and die with them; they wept [Muslim 1780, the long narration of 'Abdullah ibn Rabah from Abu Hurayrah].
- He entered almost without fighting, found 360 idols around the Ka'bah and struck them with a stick, reciting Q 17:81 [Bukhari 4287].
- He forgave old enemies, among them Hind, Wahshi and 'Ikrimah ibn Abi Jahl [Yaqeen, Confronting Hate] (single source; "Yaqeen reports"). Wahshi had killed Hamzah at Uhud and later became Muslim [Bukhari 4072].
- He never took revenge for himself [Bukhari 3560]; Day 15 already teaches "he took no revenge".

**Likely questions**
- *"Did he really say 'No blame on you today; go, you are free'?"* It's a well-known line from the early biographies, but its chain isn't confirmed as sound, so this track doesn't put it on screen. Say: "It's a well-known story whose chain scholars consider weak. What's certain is that he took no revenge [Bukhari 3560] and offered safety [Muslim 1780]." `[SCHOLAR: Q43]`
- *"Was there no fighting at all?"* Say "almost without fighting", as the sources do, and don't describe any clashes. His rule protecting women and children held [Bukhari 3015]. `[SCHOLAR: Q39]`
- *"Did he forgive Wahshi completely?"* Wahshi became Muslim and was not punished; the Prophet ﷺ asked him to keep out of his sight, the grief of a man who had lost his uncle [Bukhari 4072]. Present it honestly: forgiveness with human grief, not anger.
- *"Why were there idols at the Ka'bah?"* Over the centuries the Arabs added idol worship to Ibrahim's religion (seerah-01). The Ka'bah was built for Allah alone; Muslims don't worship it.
- *"How do I forgive someone who hurt me?"* Forgiveness doesn't erase boundaries or safety. Start with du'a, then small steps. If someone is abusive or dangerous, the safety rule comes first; don't advise reconnecting.

**Escalate:** abusive or unsafe family situations (safety rule); any question about fighting today (Day 71 frame and the imam).

**Don't**
- Don't quote "No blame on you today" as his words or put it on a screen. `[SCHOLAR: Q43]`
- Don't describe the clashes or Khalid's sector.
- Don't compare the convert's family or their faith to the idols of Makkah.
- Don't tell a convert they must reconcile with someone who is unsafe.
