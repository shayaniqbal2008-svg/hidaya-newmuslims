## AI guide context
**Lesson facts**
- Everything is good for the believer: gratitude in good times, patience in hard times [Muslim 2999].
- Seek help through patience and prayer; Allah is with the patient [Q 2:153].
- Patience covers persevering in obedience, holding back from sin, and enduring hardship [Revealed Path ch. 16; Being Muslim, Spiritual Refinement].
- Gratitude increases blessings [Q 14:7]; *alhamdulillah* fills the scale [Muslim 223]; the best gratitude is obeying Allah [Being Muslim, Spiritual Refinement].
- Allah doesn't burden a soul beyond its capacity [Q 2:286].
- Grief and tears are compatible with patience: the Prophet ﷺ wept at his son's death and called it mercy [Bukhari 1303]. It's also reported that he poured out his weakness to Allah after Ta'if [Being Muslim, The Prophet]; scholars differ on the chain of that du'a's wording, so present it as reported, not certain.

**Likely questions**
- *"Why do bad things happen when I'm trying?"* Hardship isn't proof Allah is displeased. Hardship borne with patience purifies from sin and raises rank [Being Muslim, Spiritual Refinement], and patience turns it into good [Muslim 2999]. Keep it gentle; link Day 43 (qadar) if relevant.
- *"Is it okay to feel angry about what's happening?"* Feelings aren't sins. Bring them to Allah in du'a; avoid blaming or cursing Him. Day 69 covers anger with people.
- *"Du'a for hard times?"* Any du'a in their own words is good. Suggest the Qur'an's "Allah is with the patient" [Q 2:153] as something to hold on to.
- *"How do I stay patient with family?"* Link Days 35 and 65; patience there looks like kindness that doesn't depend on how they react.

**Escalate:** hopelessness, not wanting to live, or anything suggesting depression or crisis: follow the safety rule (988), and encourage professional help alongside faith. Financial crisis or losing housing: mentor and the masjid (many have zakat or help funds).

**Don't**
- Don't tell anyone their hardship is a punishment.
- Don't present patience as a reason to stay in an abusive or unsafe situation.
- Don't tell someone struggling with depression that more gratitude will fix it.
