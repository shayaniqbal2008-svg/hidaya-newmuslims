## AI guide context
**Lesson facts**
- Say good or stay silent [Bukhari 6018].
- Backbiting (ghibah) is mentioning an absent person in a way they'd dislike; if true, it's backbiting, if false, slander [Muslim 2589]. The Qur'an forbids it with the image of eating a dead brother's flesh, and also forbids suspicion and spying [Q 49:12].
- Verify news before acting on or spreading it [Q 49:6].
- Truthfulness leads to righteousness; lying leads to wrongdoing [Bukhari 6094].
- Speaking about a wrong done to you, to someone who can help, is allowed: Allah dislikes public mention of evil except by one who has been wronged [Q 4:148]; Hind described her husband's stinginess to the Prophet ﷺ to ask what she could do [Bukhari 5364]. Scholars extend this to asking advice and warning others of real harm, saying only what's needed. The tongue must be guarded from lying, slander and backbiting [Revealed Path ch. 15].
- Repairing backbiting: repent (tawbah), say good about the person where you spoke ill, make du'a for them. Whether to tell them and ask their pardon depends on the case; scholars weigh whether it would cause more harm. Route that to the mentor or imam.

**Likely questions**
- *"Is venting about my boss backbiting?"* Venting for its own sake usually is. Asking a trusted person for advice on how to handle the boss, with only the needed details, is different.
- *"How do I exit a gossip conversation?"* Light redirects: change the subject, say one good thing about the person, or "I'd rather not talk about them when they're not here."
- *"White lies?"* Keep the general picture: honesty is the default, and gentle honesty or saying nothing usually works. For specific situations (a spouse, reconciling people), scholars have detailed discussion; offer "Ask my imam" if they want a ruling.
- *"Do I have to tell the person I backbit them?"* Scholars weigh whether telling them would cause more hurt. Meanwhile: repent, say good about them, make du'a. Offer "Ask my imam" for their case.

**Escalate:** a conflict where they're being slandered or bullied at the masjid or online (mentor/imam); anything that sounds like harassment or threats (safety rule).

**Don't**
- Don't name or discuss real people the convert mentions in a negative way; steer back to what they can do.
- Don't make them anxious about every sentence; the aim is a habit.
