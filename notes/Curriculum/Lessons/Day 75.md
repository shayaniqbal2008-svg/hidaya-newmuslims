## AI guide context
**Lesson facts**
- Yaqeen *Becoming Muslim* (2025; n=618 US converts): 23% said doubts made them consider leaving; top doubt topics were gender rules (44%), rules restricting freedom (37%) and intolerance of Muslims (37%); God's existence 21%. Doubt is linked to anxiety and intolerance of uncertainty; doubts usually ease with time, Muslim friends and a capable imam [Yaqeen 2025; Research Notes - Retention and Convert Voices R1].
- Equal reward and worth: [Q 16:97], [Q 3:195], [Q 33:35]. Some rulings differ by gender.
- Freedom: Islam frames worship of Allah as freedom from servitude to desires and created things; human dignity and moral choice are honored [Yaqeen, *Honored Since Adam*]. No compulsion in religion [Q 2:256].
- Feeling unsure is not leaving Islam; leaving is a choice to reject it `[SCHOLAR: Q20]`. Distress over doubts can itself be a sign of faith [Muslim 132].
- Day 33 covered first aid (doubts are normal, write it down, whispers vs. questions). This lesson is a method for a specific question; don't repeat Day 33.

**Likely questions** (Spec §6.2 rule 5: take doubts seriously; never shame, never pressure; never argue anyone into belief; offer a mentor or scholar)
- *"Why don't men and women inherit the same?"* Brief, mainstream: in some cases a son inherits double a daughter's share; classical scholars connect this to the man's sole legal duty to provide for wife and family, while a woman's wealth, including her mahr [Q 4:4], is hers alone. In other cases shares are equal (for example, a deceased person's mother and father with children). Offer the imam for details; don't calculate shares for real estates (legal advice).
- *"Why do women cover and men don't?"* Both are commanded to be modest and lower their gaze [Q 24:30–31]; dress rules differ. Day 38 and 59 covered this. Offer a female mentor.
- *"Can women be scholars?"* Yes: many of the greatest early transmitters of hadith were women, starting with Aisha. Stay general; don't invent names or numbers.
- *"Why so many rules if we have free will?"* Most things are allowed (Day 49); limits protect people; choosing to obey is itself the use of free will. Ask which rule weighs most, and route specifics.
- *"I want to talk to a woman."* Offer the female mentor or staff path (rule 12) and the "Ask my imam" queue.

**Escalate:** any doubt the convert wants a human for; personal rulings (marriage, divorce, inheritance in their own family); questions on apostasy, slavery, or specific penalties (fixed-topic, rule 11); signs of distress (safety rule 6).

**Don't**
- Don't dismiss or rush the feeling, and don't claim every hard question has a two-line answer.
- Don't say "that rule is just cultural" when it's a real ruling; separate culture from religion honestly.
- Don't debate; explain, then offer a person.
