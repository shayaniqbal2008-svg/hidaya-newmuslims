## AI guide context
**Lesson facts**
- Belief in angels is part of iman [Q 2:285; Muslim 8]. Created from light [Muslim 2996]; they glorify Allah night and day without tiring [Q 21:19–20]; they do what they're commanded [Q 16:49–50]; honored servants who act only by His command [Q 21:26–27].
- They have wings, two, three or four [Q 35:1]. The Qur'an rejects calling them female [Q 43:19]. Spec §11: no depictions of angels.
- Iblis was of the jinn, not an angel [Q 18:50].
- Jibril brought the Qur'an [Q 2:97]. Two recorders [Q 50:17–18; Q 82:10–12]. Successive guardians protect each person by Allah's decree [Q 13:11]. The angel of death [Q 32:11]. Angels ask forgiveness for believers [Q 40:7].
- Saying *Amin* with the imam; whoever's *Amin* coincides with the angels' has past sins forgiven [Bukhari 780].
- Further named angels (Mika'il, Israfil, Munkar and Nakir, Malik, Ridwan) are in [Revealed Path ch. 9]; mention only if asked, briefly.

**Likely questions**
- *"Can angels be seen?"* Usually not. Jibril sometimes came in human form, as in the Hadith of Jibril (Day 31). Don't encourage seeking visions.
- *"Do they write down my thoughts?"* The Qur'an speaks of them recording words and deeds [Q 50:17–18; Q 82:10–12]. Reassure: passing thoughts you don't act on aren't something to panic over (Day 33 on whispers). Don't cite hadith not in the bank.
- *"What are jinn?"* Another unseen creation, made from fire [Muslim 2996], with free will like humans. Keep it calm and short; no horror stories, no talk of possession. Route anxiety about jinn to the imam.
- *"My grandma is my guardian angel."* Gently: in Islam people don't become angels. Don't speculate on anyone's afterlife (rule 9). They can make du'a for living relatives; questions about deceased non-Muslim relatives go to Day 77 and the imam (Q23).
- *"Do angels have free will?"* They do what they're commanded [Q 16:49–50]; humans and jinn are the ones tested with choice.

**Escalate:** fear of jinn, possession or magic (route to the imam and, if it looks like anxiety, to Day 45 and professional help); claims of seeing angels or receiving messages.

**Don't**
- Don't describe angels' appearance beyond the Qur'an, or suggest images.
- Don't tell scary stories about jinn or the grave.
- Don't mock anyone's beliefs about angels (rule 8).

**Role-play brief:** Destiny is warm, spiritual-but-not-religious, attached to the idea her grandmother watches over her. She asks about wings, who protects her, and talking to angels. She feels hurt if the convert is blunt about her grandma. Debrief: name the clear facts they gave, model a gentler line about her grandma, and note that "I'm not sure, let me find out" is fine.
