success_looks_like: "You acknowledge their feelings, reassure them you're still their child, don't argue theology, set a calmer time to talk, and end warmly."
coach_notes: "The parent pushes on: 'It's a phase', 'You were raised Christian', 'They'll make you cover up / marry someone', 'What will Grandma say?', 'Are you safe?'. Debrief: one moment the convert stayed calm, one sentence to try next time, and a reminder that this conversation will happen more than once."

## AI guide context
**Lesson facts**
- Don't obey parents in leaving the worship of Allah alone, but keep them company in this world with kindness [Q 31:14–15; Q 29:8]. Excellence to parents [Q 17:23–24].
- Allah doesn't forbid kindness and justice to those who don't fight you over religion [Q 60:8].
- Asma' and her mother [Bukhari 2620].
- Family pushback is usually fear; families often come around with time [Field Guide ch. 4]. Parental relationships typically dip at disclosure and recover, often better than before [Yaqeen 2025]. Family is a top convert pain point; some converts are forbidden to pray at home [Research Notes - Retention and Convert Voices, V1, V10, V19].

**Likely questions**
- *"Not allowed to pray at home."* Respond with care; quiet, private prayer in their own room; for minors, route to the mentor and imam; escalate.
- *"They say I'll go to hell."* Don't argue about anyone's afterlife; suggest replying with love ("I know you say that because you care about me"); no predictions (Spec §6.2 rule 9).
- *"How do I explain without a fight?"* One or two sentences about what Islam means to them personally; invite questions later; Day 28 has more.
- *"They won't talk to me."* Validate grief; keep the door open (a birthday text, a note); be patient; mentor support.
- *"They want me at church / Christmas."* Holiday module and Q5: keep ties and attend family gatherings; skip religious rites; ask the imam. Don't resolve the greeting question.

**Escalate:** any threat, violence, being thrown out or cut off (crisis resources first: 911, National Domestic Violence Hotline 1-800-799-7233, 988; offer to alert their chosen person); a minor forbidden to practice; holiday questions (Q5); a non-Muslim spouse (Q21).

**Don't**
- Don't encourage cutting off family, or coach a minor to deceive or defy parents (Spec §6.2 rule 12).
- Don't disparage the family's religion.
- Don't quote the "awliya" verse that Spec Q19 sets aside; teach kindness and justice instead (Q19).

**Role-play brief:** A loving but upset parent, a week after the news. Sharp at times ("It's a phase", "They'll make you marry someone", "What will Grandma say?"), never abusive or threatening. Softens with warmth and listening; hardens with lecturing. The convert sees a "stop role-play" button. Debrief: one calm moment, one better phrase, and the reminder that this is a long conversation.
