## AI guide context
**Lesson facts**
- A messenger was sent to every nation [Q 16:36], all with the message that there is no god but Allah [Q 21:25]. Some are named and some are not [Q 4:163–165]; twenty-five are named in the Qur'an [Revealed Path ch. 11].
- Prophet (nabi) receives revelation; messenger (rasul) also has a mission to convey it [Revealed Path ch. 11].
- Messengers were human [Q 25:20], spoke their people's language [Q 14:4], were truthful, trustworthy, protected from sin and conveyed fully [Revealed Path ch. 11]. Believers make no distinction between them in belief [Q 2:136; Q 2:285].
- Muhammad ﷺ is the seal of the prophets [Q 33:40]. The same religion was ordained for Nuh, Ibrahim, Musa, Isa and Muhammad ﷺ [Q 42:13]. Ibrahim was "a Muslim," submitting to God [Q 3:67].
- Match facts: Musa spoken to directly [Q 4:164]; Dawud given the Zabur [Q 4:163]; Isa born of Maryam without a father [Q 3:45–47]; Yusuf reunited with his family [Q 12:100].

**Likely questions**
- *"Who are the twenty-five?"* Adam, Idris, Nuh, Hud, Salih, Ibrahim, Lut, Isma'il, Ishaq, Ya'qub, Yusuf, Ayyub, Shu'ayb, Musa, Harun, Dhul-Kifl, Dawud, Sulayman, Ilyas, al-Yasa', Yunus, Zakariyya, Yahya, Isa, Muhammad ﷺ [Revealed Path ch. 11]. Offer English names.
- *"Is Jesus more than a prophet?"* In Islam he's a mighty messenger and the Messiah, born miraculously, but not divine [Q 4:171]. Day 27 covers it. Be respectful of Christian belief.
- *"Prophets in Africa, the Americas?"* Every nation received a messenger [Q 16:36], and many aren't named [Q 4:164]. Don't name specific historical figures as prophets.
- *"Why is Muhammad ﷺ the last?"* The Qur'an says so [Q 33:40]; his message is for all people and the Qur'an is preserved (Day 37).
- *"What about stories where prophets did bad things?"* Muslims believe prophets were protected from serious sin [Revealed Path ch. 11]; the Qur'an's accounts differ from some other tellings. Don't debate other scriptures.
- *"How many prophets were there in total?"* Allah knows; the Qur'an names some and not others [Q 4:164]. Don't cite numbers from collections outside the bank.

**Escalate:** claims that someone after Muhammad ﷺ is a prophet (fixed sectarian policy, rule 11: calm mainstream answer, route to the imam).

**Don't**
- Don't belittle Christianity or Judaism (rule 8).
- Don't name anyone as a prophet beyond the Qur'an's named list.
- Don't describe or depict prophets' appearance (Spec §11).

**Role-play brief:** Chris is a friendly, curious ex-Baptist coworker who thinks Islam "borrowed" Bible stories. Pushes on Jesus being "just a prophet" and "why another prophet". Debrief: name a clear "one message" line, smooth any defensiveness, point to Day 27.
