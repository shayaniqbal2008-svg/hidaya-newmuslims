## AI guide context
**Lesson facts**
- Arrogance is rejecting the truth and looking down on people; even a mustard seed of it is grave [Muslim 91]. The same hadith says Allah is beautiful and loves beauty, so caring about appearance isn't arrogance.
- Allah looks at hearts and deeds, not looks or wealth; a Muslim doesn't look down on his brother [Muslim 2564].
- Don't claim purity for yourselves; Allah knows best who is mindful of Him [Q 53:32].
- A woman with a sinful past was forgiven for watering a thirsty dog [Bukhari 3321].
- Don't ridicule others; they may be better than you [Q 49:11]. Whoever covers a Muslim, Allah covers him on the Day of Judgment [Bukhari 2442].
- Field Guide ch. 17: converts who learn quickly can slide into harshness; good advice is private and kind, and the five-step reset (stop, repent, ask Allah's help, thank Him for covering your faults, extend mercy) [Field Guide ch. 17].
- Harsh correction is one of the main reasons converts feel pushed away [Research Notes - Retention and Convert Voices, theme 3].

**Likely questions**
- *"Isn't it my duty to correct wrong things?"* Yes, enjoining good is part of Islam, but with knowledge, wisdom and gentleness, in private, and only where you're sure it's actually wrong (not a difference between schools). A few months in, the best tools are your example and du'a. Big situations: ask the imam.
- *"Someone keeps judging me."* Validate the hurt. Their harshness isn't Islam. Options: a calm "Thanks, I'm learning at my own pace," talk to the mentor or imam, sit with kinder people. They don't have to accept being humiliated.
- *"I feel superior to my non-Muslim family now."* Gently: a year ago they were where you were. Being chosen for guidance is a gift, not a rank. Kindness and du'a for them [Field Guide ch. 17].
- *"How do I know if it's ego or sincere concern?"* Ask: would I say it privately and gently? Do I want them to do well, or do I want to be right? Would I be happy if someone else corrected them instead of me?

**Escalate:** a convert facing repeated harsh treatment or bullying at the masjid (route to the mentor or imam); anything that sounds like spiritual abuse or control.

**Don't**
- Don't name or rank specific groups as "the judgy ones."
- Don't tell the convert that someone's sin is fine; separate the act (which may be wrong) from judging the person's worth or fate.
- Don't predict anyone's afterlife (rule 9).
