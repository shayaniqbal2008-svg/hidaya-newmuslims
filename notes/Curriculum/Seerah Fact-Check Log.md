# Seerah Fact-Check Log

> [!info] What this is
> One row per key fact in [[Seerah Outline]] (IDs match the outline). First pass by the outline researcher on **2026-10-08**. **A later independent agent re-verifies every row** (Practice hub spec §6 and §8): it re-opens each source, adds its own verdict in a second pass, and adds rows for any new claims the episode writers introduce. Nothing ships with an unresolved row.
>
> **How the first pass was done:** every Qur'an verse and hadith was checked with `Tools/cite.py` against the full text (not just the opening lines), and all of them are in the [[Citation Bank]] (86 hadith and 32 verses added today; non-Bukhari/Muslim grades recorded; only sahih and hasan are used as fact). Present-day sources were opened with curl or WebFetch, and the specific claim was found in the page text. Source tags are defined in [[Seerah Outline#Source key (all opened 2026-10-08)]].
>
> **Verdicts:** `confirmed` means a primary text (Qur'an or sahih/hasan hadith) or ≥2 independent present-day sources say it. `cautious wording` means the fact is true as far as sources go, but there's only one present-day text source, or the wording must hedge ("about", "the early biographers report"). `disputed-logged` means sources differ; the outline uses the majority or cautious form, and the difference is noted here. `weak-excluded` means it's left out of episodes (see the second table).

**First-pass totals:** 207 key facts: 158 confirmed, 41 cautious wording, 8 disputed-logged; plus 22 weak or excluded reports in the second table.

## 3. Differences between sources (logged for the verifier)

- **Khadijah's age at marriage** (06.3): 40 (IQA-11575; QI-EP21, "it is said") vs 28 to mid-30s (QI-EP21, some scholars). Outline: "older than him; most reports say forty".
- **Year of Sorrow** (16.2): 10th year of the mission (IQA-11575) vs 11th (QI-EP55/56). Outline: "about ten years in".
- **Night Journey timing** (18.6): 1 year (IQA-84314, DIE-Isra), 1 year 2 months (Ibn 'Abd al-Barr via IQA-84314), 1.5 years (IQA-145725) before the hijra.
- **Stay at Quba** (22.2): 10 nights (Bukhari 3906), 14 nights (Bukhari 428), "over ten" (IQA-11575).
- **The masjid's land** (23.1): two orphans' date yard (Bukhari 3906) vs Banu al-Najjar refusing payment (Bukhari 428).
- **The Trench's year** (27.1): 5 AH (IQA-11575 sequence) vs Shawwal 4 AH (Musa ibn 'Uqbah, in Sahih al-Bukhari's chapter heading; not fetched today, so the verifier should confirm).
- **Hudaybiyah numbers** (29.1): 1,400 (Bukhari 4154, YQ-Hate); Bukhari has 1,500 in other reports (not fetched today).
- **Birth year** (02.2): 570 (YQ-WIS) vs 571 (QI-EP9, "25th April 571"); IQA-147601 gives both. DIE-167 gives "20 April 570, 50 days before the year of the elephant", which conflicts with IQA-147601's consensus that he was born *in* the Year of the Elephant. Outline: "about 570".
- **Death date** (39.1): 12 Rabi' al-Awwal (IQA-147601 "most well-known"; YQ-WIS "many held") vs other days. Outline: "Rabi' al-Awwal 11 AH".
- **Hilf al-Fudul** (05.3): a pact to defend the wronged (QI-EP19; DIE-174) vs "a peace treaty after the Fijar war" (DIE-168).
- **After Khadijah** (40.6): YQ-WIS says he "remained unmarried for several years", but the sirah order (Sawdah soon after) suggests otherwise. Outline avoids the claim.
- **Envoys to the Negus** (13.3): 'Amr ibn al-'As with 'Ammarah (QI-EP43) vs with 'Abdullah ibn Abi Rabi'ah (DIE-175). Outline names only 'Amr, or neither.
