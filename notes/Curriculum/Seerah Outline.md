# Seerah Outline (41 episodes)

> [!info] What this is
> The factual backbone for the optional seerah track (Practice hub spec §6, repo `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-07-practice-hub-design.md`). Writers draft `Lessons/Seerah/seerah-NN.md` (format 2, [[Curriculum Spec]] §4.6) from this page. **Use only the facts listed here, worded with the cautions given.** Every hadith and verse below is in the [[Citation Bank]] (added 2026-10-08, "Used on" = `seerah-NN`). Every key fact has a numbered row in the [[Seerah Fact-Check Log]], which an independent agent will re-verify before anything ships.

## How to read an episode

- **Hook**: the one line that opens the episode.
- **Key facts** (4–8). Each has an ID (e.g. `12.3`, used in the fact-check log) and its source:
  - Qur'an and hadith refs are in the [[Citation Bank]]. Bukhari and Muslim are sahih; other collections show their grade.
  - `[Seerah: …]` means the event is known only from the classical biographies (Ibn Ishaq/Ibn Hisham, Ibn Sa'd, al-Tabari). It is backed by at least two present-day sources from the key below. Tell it with "according to the early biographers" or "about" wherever the log says *cautious wording*.
- **Lesson today**: the character lesson for a new Muslim. It's about character, not rulings.
- **Careful**: disputed dates and numbers, weak stories to leave out or label, and sensitive topics, with the `[SCHOLAR: Qn]` flags (Q37–Q45, added to [[Curriculum Spec]] §9 and the [[Scholar Review Packet]]).
- **Builds on**: the 90-day lessons that already teach part of this. Link to them; don't re-teach them.

**Ground rules for every episode**
1. Hadith are paraphrased in our own words, never quoted as his exact words. Short verses may be quoted in Saheeh International, with attribution.
2. Never picture the Prophet ﷺ, angels or prophets. Scenes show places (the Ka'bah, a cave, a desert road, a masjid) and icons. Faceless people are fine for Companions in crowd scenes, but there are no Companions as role-play characters ([[Curriculum Spec]] §6.2 rule 13).
3. Battles are told as history: why they happened, how he behaved, what the Qur'an said about them. Never use graphic detail, and never glorify killing. Each battle episode carries his rules of war [Bukhari 3015].
4. Sensitive topics (marriages, Aisha's age, Banu Qurayzah, slavery and captives, the fate of relatives who weren't Muslim) are never debated on screen. Where the outline calls for a calm line, use it. Detail lives in the episode's `AI guide context`, with "Ask my imam" offered.
5. Weak stories are left out of screens. If the AI guide is asked, it calls them "a well-known story whose chain scholars consider weak" (or "…whose chain scholars differ on", where the log says *disputed*).
6. Dates: give CE years as "about", and AH years where the log confirms them. Don't give exact days unless the log marks them confirmed.
7. Suggested widgets: `timeline` (for most episodes), `masjid-map` (seerah-23), `moon` (seerah-24 and seerah-37), `qibla` (seerah-24). The `timeline` widget is ideal for a running "where we are" strip.

## Part 1 · Before the message

### seerah-01 · Arabia and the House of Ibrahim
**Hook:** Long before Islam, a valley in the desert already had a House built for one God.
**Key facts**
- `01.1` Ibrahim and Isma'il raised the foundations of the House (the Ka'bah) and prayed for it to be accepted [Q 2:127].
- `01.2` By the Prophet's ﷺ time it was surrounded by idols: 360 were around it when Makkah was opened [Bukhari 4287].
- `01.3` The Arabs kept parts of Ibrahim's religion (honouring the Ka'bah, tawaf, Hajj and 'Umrah, sacrifice) but had added idol worship, and some customs were cruel (burying daughters, usury, blood feuds) [Seerah: IQA-11575; DIE-167].
- `01.4` 'Amr ibn Luhayy is reported to be the first to bring idols to Makkah [Seerah: IQA-11575; QI-EP4] *(cautious wording)*.
- `01.5` Makkah was a trading town run by the tribe of Quraysh, whose caravans went to Syria and Yemen [Seerah: DIE-167; QI-EP8].
- `01.6` Ibrahim settled some of his descendants in an uncultivated valley near the sacred House, praying they would establish prayer [Q 14:37]. *(Added by the episode writer, 2026-10-09.)*
**Lesson today:** People can keep the outer shell of a good tradition and lose its heart. Islam came to restore worship of the One God that was already there.
**Careful:** Don't describe pre-Islamic Arabs as all bad: IQA-11575 also lists their generosity, loyalty and courage. Say so; it models fairness to a convert's own pre-Islam culture.
**Builds on:** Day 47 (Hajj and Umrah: the Ka'bah), modules eid-adha (Ibrahim and Isma'il, Bukhari 3364), Day 39 (the messengers).

### seerah-02 · The Year of the Elephant
**Hook:** An army with an elephant marched on the Ka'bah. It never arrived. That same year, a boy named Muhammad was born.
**Key facts**
- `02.1` An army "of the elephant" came to destroy the Ka'bah and Allah turned it back with flocks of birds [Q 105:1–5].
- `02.2` The Prophet ﷺ was born in Makkah in that Year of the Elephant, about 570 CE (some calculate 571) [Seerah: IQA-147601; YQ-WIS; QI-EP9].
- `02.3` He was born on a Monday [Muslim 1162].
- `02.4` His lineage: Muhammad son of 'Abdullah son of 'Abd al-Muttalib, of Banu Hashim of Quraysh, descended from Isma'il [Muslim 2276; IQA-289868].
- `02.5` His father 'Abdullah died before he was born [Seerah: IQA-11575; QI-EP8; DIE-168].
- `02.6` His grandfather 'Abd al-Muttalib named him Muhammad ("the much-praised") [Seerah: QI-EP9 only] *(cautious wording: "the early biographers say"; second source needed, see log)*.
**Lesson today:** Allah protects what is His, often in ways no one expects. Neither Allah's plan nor anyone's worth depended on having a father around.
**Careful:** The **day and month are disputed** (2nd, 8th, 9th, 10th, 12th or 17th Rabi' al-Awwal; IQA-147601 says the strongest is 8th–12th). Say "in the month of Rabi' al-Awwal, by most accounts", not a date [SCHOLAR: Q44]. Leave out the wonders reported at his birth (the Persian palace shaking, the fire going out): QI-EP10 notes their authenticity is questioned. Don't discuss celebrating the Mawlid; the AI guide sends that question to the imam (Q44).
**Builds on:** Day 15 (born in Makkah c. 570).

### seerah-03 · Halimah and the desert
**Hook:** No nurse wanted the orphan. The one who took him found her whole year changed.
**Key facts**
- `03.1` Makkan families sent babies to be nursed in the healthier desert, where they would also learn pure Arabic [Seerah: QI-EP12; DIE-168].
- `03.2` Halimah al-Sa'diyyah of Banu Sa'd nursed him [Seerah: IQA-11575; QI-EP12; DIE-168].
- `03.3` She took him only because no other baby was left: the nurses passed over an orphan who couldn't pay well. Afterwards, she said, her family's fortunes turned [Seerah: QI-EP12; DIE-168] *(cautious wording: "Halimah later said…")*.
- `03.4` Before Halimah, he was also nursed by Thuwaybah, who also nursed his uncle Hamzah and Abu Salamah [Bukhari 5101 (Abu Salamah); QI-EP44 (Hamzah)].
- `03.5` While he was a boy playing with other children, Jibril came, opened his chest and washed his heart with Zamzam [Muslim 162].
- `03.6` Halimah said she did not want to go home without a baby, so she took the orphan [Seerah: QI-EP12; DIE-168] *(cautious wording: "Halimah later said"; added 2026-10-09)*. Muslim 162 also says the other boys ran to his nurse and found him safe.
**Lesson today:** The world overlooked the orphan; Allah didn't. Being passed over by people says nothing about your worth.
**Careful:** Tell `03.5` simply, as the hadith does: no gore, no pictures. The details of the chest-opening story vary (where, at what age); give no age. Barakah (Umm Ayman), his carer, was an Abyssinian woman whom he inherited and freed [QI-EP11]; if she's mentioned, say "freed" plainly (Q40).
**Builds on:** none directly.

### seerah-04 · Orphaned twice
**Hook:** By eight, he had lost his father, his mother and his grandfather.
**Key facts**
- `04.1` At about six he travelled with his mother Aminah to visit relatives in Madinah. On the way back she died at al-Abwa' [Seerah: IQA-11575; QI-EP14].
- `04.2` Umm Ayman (Barakah) brought him home [Seerah: QI-EP14] *(cautious wording)*.
- `04.3` His grandfather 'Abd al-Muttalib took him in and loved him dearly. He died when the boy was about eight [Seerah: IQA-20240; IQA-11575; QI-EP15].
- `04.4` His uncle Abu Talib, his father's full brother, then raised him. He was poor but loved him like his own children, and protected him for over forty years [Seerah: IQA-20240; IQA-11575; QI-EP15].
- `04.5` The Qur'an later reminded him: Did He not find you an orphan and give you shelter? [Q 93:6–8]
- `04.6` Umm Ayman (Barakah) was an Abyssinian woman in his family's household whom he inherited and later freed [Seerah: QI-EP11] *(cautious wording; Q40; added 2026-10-09 from this episode's Careful note)*.
**Lesson today:** Allah never left him without someone who loved him. Converts who lose family closeness can trust that Allah sends people.
**Careful:** **Don't discuss the afterlife of his parents or Abu Talib.** Sahih hadith exist [Muslim 976 (AI guide only); Bukhari 1360] and are painful for converts with non-Muslim parents. Not on screen. The AI guide answers gently, doesn't deny the texts, never speculates about any individual, and offers the imam [SCHOLAR: Q42] (see Q23).
**Builds on:** Day 15 (orphan, raised by grandfather then uncle), Day 77 (grief; Abu Talib and Q 9:113).

### seerah-05 · Shepherd, trader, and a pact for justice
**Hook:** Before anyone called him a prophet, they called him "the trustworthy".
**Key facts**
- `05.1` He herded sheep for the people of Makkah for small wages, as every prophet herded sheep [Bukhari 2262].
- `05.2` As a young man he worked in trade [Seerah: IQA-11575; QI-EP20; DIE-169].
- `05.3` Around age twenty he witnessed *Hilf al-Fudul*, a pact of Makkan clans to stand up for anyone wronged in Makkah, prompted by a visiting trader who had been cheated [Seerah: QI-EP19; DIE-174 (an alliance to support the wronged); DIE-168 mentions the pact after the Fijar war] *(cautious wording: "about twenty")*.
- `05.4` His people called him al-Amin, "the trustworthy" [Seerah: IQA-11575; QI-EP23].
- `05.5` He never worshipped idols, and he avoided alcohol and immoral behaviour [Seerah: IQA-11575; DIE-167 (he said he never worshipped al-Lat and al-'Uzza)].
- `05.6` The cheated trader (of Zubayd) called out for help at the Ka'bah; clans met at 'Abdullah ibn Jud'an's house and swore the pact; the goods were returned [Seerah: QI-EP19] *(cautious wording: "the early biographers say"; single source; added 2026-10-09)*.
**Lesson today:** Character comes before a title. Converts can be "al-Amin" at work and school long before they know much fiqh.
**Careful:** The **monk Bahira** story (a monk in Syria recognising the boy as a future prophet) is **left out**. Its hadith form is Tirmidhi 3620, graded munkar or da'if in our dataset (Shakir, al-Albani, Zubair Ali Zai), and QI-EP16 notes the narrations differ. If asked: "a well-known story whose chain scholars consider weak." The saying that he would answer the Fudul pact's call even in Islam is in Ahmad/al-Bayhaqi (outside the six), so don't quote it; describe the pact only.
**Builds on:** Day 15 (al-Amin; "Revealed Path ch. 11").

### seerah-06 · Khadijah
**Hook:** She was a successful businesswoman. She hired him, watched him, and then proposed.
**Key facts**
- `06.1` Khadijah bint Khuwaylid, a respected and wealthy Makkan widow, hired him to take her goods to Syria, and he made a good profit [Seerah: IQA-11575; IQA-34550; QI-EP20].
- `06.2` Impressed by his honesty, she proposed through a friend (Nafisah) [Seerah: IQA-34550; QI-EP21].
- `06.3` He was about 25. She is usually said to have been 40, though some reports give a younger age (28 to mid-30s) [Seerah: IQA-11575; QI-EP21; YQ-WIS] *(disputed, logged)*.
- `06.4` He married no one else while she lived [Muslim 2436].
- `06.5` Years after her death he still spoke of her often and sent gifts to her friends; Aisha said she was jealous of no one as much as of Khadijah [Bukhari 3818].
- `06.6` Jibril brought Khadijah greetings of peace from her Lord and good news of a house in Paradise [Bukhari 3820].
**Lesson today:** A marriage built on character and respect, and loyalty that outlives death. It also shows a woman's initiative and business, honoured.
**Careful:** The ages are disputed: say "about 25" and "older than him; most reports say forty". Their children are covered in seerah-40.
**Builds on:** Day 42 and Day 66 (marriage), Day 65 (family).

### seerah-07 · The Black Stone
**Hook:** Four clans were ready to fight over who would lift one stone. He found a way for all of them to lift it.
**Key facts**
- `07.1` When he was about 35, Quraysh rebuilt the damaged Ka'bah [Seerah: IQA-11575; QI-EP22].
- `07.2` He carried stones for it alongside his uncle al-'Abbas [Bukhari 364].
- `07.3` The clans quarrelled over who would set the Black Stone back in place, and agreed to let the next man to enter decide [Seerah: QI-EP23; DIE-169].
- `07.4` He was the next to come in, and they were relieved: "al-Amin has come" [Seerah: QI-EP23; IQA-11575].
- `07.5` He placed the stone on a cloth, had a leader of each clan lift an edge together, and then set it in place himself [Seerah: IQA-11575; QI-EP23].
**Lesson today:** A conflict can often be solved so that everyone keeps their dignity. That's useful in families divided over a convert's new faith.
**Careful:** Whether the cloth was his own garment varies ("some say"). Say "a cloth".
**Builds on:** Day 15 (al-Amin).

## Part 2 · Makkah: the message

### seerah-08 · Hira: "Read!"
**Hook:** A man alone in a mountain cave hears a voice: "Read!" He answers, "I can't read."
**Key facts**
- `08.1` He began to love solitude and would spend nights worshipping in the cave of Hira [Bukhari 3].
- `08.2` The angel came and told him to read; he said he could not; the first revelation was the opening of Surah al-'Alaq [Bukhari 3; Q 96:1–5].
- `08.3` He was forty when revelation began [Bukhari 3902], about 610 CE [Seerah: YQ-WIS (born 570, + 40); DIE-170].
- `08.4` Revelation began on a Monday [Muslim 1162], in Ramadan according to most scholars [Seerah: QI-EP28; Q 2:185 (in bank)] *(cautious wording: the day of the month is disputed)*.
- `08.5` He came home shaking and asked to be covered. Khadijah reassured him: Allah would never disgrace a man who kept family ties, helped the weak and honoured guests [Bukhari 3].
- `08.6` She took him to her cousin Waraqah ibn Nawfal, an elderly Christian who knew the scripture, who said it was the same angel who came to Musa [Bukhari 3].
- `08.7` Waraqah warned that his people would drive him out, and said he'd support him if he lived [Bukhari 3]. *(added 2026-10-09 (writer 06-17))*
- `08.8` A few days later Waraqah died, and revelation paused for a while [Bukhari 3]. *(added 2026-10-09 (writer 06-17))*
**Lesson today:** The first word of Islam was "Read." His wife's support at the hardest moment is a model for anyone whose spouse or friend is changing.
**Careful:** This is already taught on Day 15. **Build, don't repeat**: open with "You met this moment on Day 15. Here's the rest of that night", and spend the screens on Khadijah and Waraqah. Waraqah was a Christian who believed him; treat Christianity respectfully (Day 27).
**Builds on:** Day 15 (Hira, Q 96:1–5, Bukhari 3), Day 19 (the Qur'an), Day 27 (Jesus and Mary).

### seerah-09 · The pause, and "Arise and warn"
**Hook:** After the first revelation, silence. Then the angel appeared again.
**Key facts**
- `09.1` There was a pause in revelation. Then, walking, he saw the angel of Hira again between the sky and the earth, went home frightened, and asked to be wrapped up [Bukhari 4].
- `09.2` Allah revealed: O you who covers himself, arise and warn, and glorify your Lord [Q 74:1–5; Bukhari 4].
- `09.3` After that, revelation came steadily [Bukhari 4; IQA-11575].
- `09.4` Another time, when he was ill and missed his night prayer for a few nights, a woman mocked that his "devil" had left him, and Surah al-Duha was revealed: your Lord has not left you, nor does He hate you [Bukhari 4950; Q 93:1–3].
**Lesson today:** Quiet spells in faith don't mean Allah has left you. This ties straight to the convert's own "iman dips."
**Careful:** **Leave out the report that he thought of throwing himself from a mountain during the pause.** It's an unconnected addition by al-Zuhri in Bukhari 6982, not part of the hadith's chain, and scholars reject it as a basis (IQA-152611). The pause's length is disputed (days to years; QI-EP29), so say "a while". Keep `09.4` separate: Bukhari 4950 is a different occasion from the first pause (some link them; don't).
**Builds on:** Day 12 and Day 25 (iman goes up and down), Day 45 (running on empty).

### seerah-10 · The first believers
**Hook:** The first people to believe him knew him best: his wife, his best friend, a boy in his house and a freed slave.
**Key facts**
- `10.1` Khadijah was the first woman to accept Islam, and by most accounts the very first believer [Seerah: SG-Khadija (first woman); DIE-172 ("the first believer after the Prophet"); QI-EP55] *(cautious wording: "the first to believe him" only with "by most accounts")*.
- `10.2` Among the first were 'Ali (a boy in his household), Zayd ibn Harithah (a former slave he had freed and taken as a son), and Abu Bakr, the first free adult man [Seerah: QI-EP32; IQA-11575].
- `10.3` For about three years the call was private, and believers met in secret, including at the house of al-Arqam [Seerah: DIE-172 (first phase three years); DIE-174 (Dar al-Arqam three years); QI-EP33] *(cautious wording: "about three years")*.

- `10.5` Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas said that for a time he was one of only three Muslims [Bukhari 3726].
**Lesson today:** Islam started tiny and was carried by the "unimportant": slaves, women, the young. A convert who feels alone is in good company.
**Careful:** "First" lists differ by category (first woman, man, child, freed slave); say "among the very first". Zayd: say he was kidnapped as a child, sold into slavery, freed and raised as a son. The adoption rule later changed [Q 33:5, not in bank; don't teach], so the AI guide only (Q40).

### seerah-11 · The call from Mount Safa
**Hook:** He climbed a hill and asked: "If I told you an army was behind this hill, would you believe me?"
**Key facts**
- `11.1` Allah commanded him to warn his nearest relatives [Q 26:214].
- `11.2` He climbed al-Safa and called the clans of Quraysh by name [Bukhari 4770].
- `11.3` He asked if they'd believe him if he warned of an army; they said they had never known him to lie; he said he was a warner before a severe punishment [Bukhari 4770].

### seerah-12 · Faith under pressure
**Hook:** They put a rock on Bilal's chest in the noon sun. He kept saying one word: "One."
**Key facts**
- `12.1` The first seven to make their Islam public included Abu Bakr, 'Ammar and his mother Sumayyah, Suhayb, Bilal and al-Miqdad. Those without tribal protection were tortured in the sun, and Bilal would only say "Ahad, Ahad" (One, One) [Ibn Majah 150, Hasan Sahih].
- `12.2` Sumayyah, 'Ammar's mother, was killed by Abu Jahl for refusing to give up Islam, the first martyr in Islam [Seerah: SG-Sumayyah; QI-EP37; YQ-Hate].
- `12.3` 'Ammar, under torture, said what they demanded; he came to the Prophet ﷺ in tears, and the Qur'an excuses one forced while the heart stays firm in faith [Q 16:106; Seerah link to 'Ammar: QI-EP37] *(cautious wording: "commentators connect this verse to 'Ammar")*.
- `12.4` The Prophet ﷺ himself was not spared: camel waste was dumped on his back while he prostrated, and his daughter Fatimah removed it [Bukhari 240]; 'Uqbah choked him with a cloth while he prayed until Abu Bakr pushed him off [Bukhari 3856].
- `12.5` When Khabbab, tortured, asked him to pray for relief, he said believers before them had endured worse, and that Allah would complete this religion [Bukhari 3852].
- `12.6` The first seven included the Prophet ﷺ himself (protected through Abu Talib) and Abu Bakr (protected through his people) [Ibn Majah 150]. *(added 2026-10-09 (writer 06-17); note: the hook's "rock on Bilal's chest" isn't in Ibn Majah 150 or any logged source, so seerah-12 uses "the burning sun")*
**Lesson today:** Faith can be held under pressure, and a person forced to say something isn't judged by it. The episode also honours enslaved and poor early Muslims as heroes.
**Careful:** **No graphic torture detail** (no iron combs or burning on screen). "Be patient, family of Yasir, your promise is Paradise" is in al-Hakim (outside the six collections); don't quote it as hadith ("the early biographers report…" only, if at all). Bilal was enslaved and Abu Bakr bought his freedom [Seerah: QI-EP37], so say it plainly (Q40). Converts facing family pressure: add `{teen}` line routing to the mentor; never present torture as something they must endure; safety first (Curriculum Spec §6 safety rule).
**Builds on:** Day 74 (Muslims in America; enslaved African Muslims), Day 64 (patience).

### seerah-13 · Refuge with a just king
**Hook:** Persecuted at home, a group of Muslims fled across the sea to a Christian king.
**Key facts**
- `13.1` He advised vulnerable Muslims to go to Abyssinia, whose king, the Negus (al-Najashi), was known for justice [Seerah: QI-EP41; DIE-175].
- `13.2` The first group was small (about 11–12 men and 4 women, including 'Uthman and the Prophet's daughter Ruqayyah); later about a hundred went [Seerah: QI-EP41; DIE-175] *(cautious wording: "a small group, then about a hundred")*.
- `13.3` Quraysh sent envoys with gifts to have them returned; the Negus heard both sides [Seerah: QI-EP43; DIE-175].
- `13.4` Ja'far ibn Abi Talib spoke for the Muslims and recited from Surah Maryam; the king was moved and refused to hand them over [Seerah: QI-EP43; DIE-175; the passage: Q 19:16–36 (in bank)] *(cautious wording: "from Surah Maryam", not which verses)*.
- `13.5` When the Negus died years later, the Prophet ﷺ announced it the same day, called him "a righteous man" and "your brother Ashamah", and led the funeral prayer for him [Bukhari 1245; Bukhari 3877].
**Lesson today:** Justice and kindness can come from people of another faith. It's a model for converts with Christian family and friends (Q 60:8).
**Careful:** Ja'far's full speech is in Musnad Ahmad (outside the six); paraphrase it as "the early reports say" and don't quote it. The Negus is understood to have become Muslim (Bukhari 3877, "your brother"). Keep it warm toward Christians; no polemics.
**Builds on:** Day 27 (Jesus and Mary; Q 19:16–36), Day 14 and Day 65 (non-Muslim family; Q 60:8).

### seerah-14 · Hamzah and 'Umar
**Hook:** Two of Makkah's strongest men, one a hunter and one a feared enforcer, joined the Muslims within days of each other.
**Key facts**
- `14.1` His uncle Hamzah, a famed hunter and warrior, heard that Abu Jahl had insulted the Prophet ﷺ, confronted him, and declared Islam [Seerah: QI-EP44; DIE-175 (both name Hamzah's Islam)] *(cautious wording)*.
- `14.2` 'Umar ibn al-Khattab, a fierce opponent, accepted Islam after hearing the Qur'an recited at his sister's house [Seerah: QI-EP45; DIE-173].

### seerah-17 · Ta'if: the hardest day
**Hook:** He walked to another town to ask for help. They chased him out with stones. Then an angel offered revenge.
**Key facts**
- `17.1` After the Year of Sorrow, he went to Ta'if to invite its people and seek support. They refused, insulted him, and set people on him who threw stones until his feet bled [Seerah: IQA-11575; YQ-Hate].

**Hook:** Assassins waited at his door. In his bed lay his young cousin.

- `20.2` He had 'Ali sleep in his bed and stay behind to return the valuables people had left with him (even his enemies trusted him) [Seerah: IQA-11575; DIE-Hijrah] *(the trusts: IQA-11575; cautious wording)*.

### seerah-41 · His character, and loving him today
**Hook:** His servant of ten years never once heard "Why did you do that?" You never met him, and he called you his brother.
**Key facts**
- `41.1` Anas served him for ten years and was never once told "uff" or blamed [Bukhari 6038].
- `41.2` He never hit a woman or a servant, and never took revenge for himself [Muslim 2328; Bukhari 3560].
- `41.3` He never spoke obscenely, and said the best of you are those with the best character [Bukhari 3559]. Aisha said his character was the Qur'an [Muslim 746]; the Qur'an says he was of great character [Q 68:4 (in bank)].
- `41.4` He helped with the housework [Bukhari 676 (in bank)] and played with children [Bukhari 6203 (in bank)].
- `41.5` He longed for "his brothers": believers who would come later and believe without seeing him [Muslim 249].
- `41.6` Faith isn't complete until you love him more than family and everyone else [Bukhari 15]. Love of Allah means following him [Q 3:31]. Allah and His angels send blessings on him, and believers are told to as well [Q 33:56].
- `41.7` He was sent as a mercy to all the worlds [Q 21:107 (in bank)], and grieves for what burdens you [Q 9:128].
- `41.8` He'd recognise later believers by the marks of wudu on their faces, arms and legs [Muslim 249] *(added 2026-10-09 (writer 30-41))*
**Lesson today:** A convert today is one of the "brothers and sisters" he longed for. Loving him looks like following his character, one habit at a time.
**Careful:** Don't pressure ("the Prophet would be disappointed in you": Day 15 rule). Bukhari 15's love isn't a feelings test; frame it as something that grows. No physical description used as a picture.
**Builds on:** Day 15 (Meet the Prophet), Day 73 (the Prophet's character), Day 26–27 (salawat; Bukhari 3370).
