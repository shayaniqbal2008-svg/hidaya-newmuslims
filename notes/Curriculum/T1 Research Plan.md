# T1 Research Plan

Approved by Shayan on 2026-10-05. Output goes to [[Research Report]].

## Structure
- Main session (Fable) orchestrates, fact-checks and writes the report. It never does bulk reading itself.
- Five Opus research agents run in parallel, one per area. Each fills in the same record for every source.
- One revision round: Fable sends agents back for gaps, then writes the report.

## Record per source
Title · author/organization (credentials, tradition) · format · length (days/weeks/episodes) · intended audience · topics in order · pacing · tone · strengths · gaps · how accessed · URL · confidence (high/medium/low).

## Source tiers
1. Established scholars and institutions: the only tier that can back religious content.
2. Reputable organizations and articles.
3. What converts say (forums, essays): used to understand needs, never for religious rulings.

## Access rules
- Free sources only. Legitimately free copies only: author/publisher releases, archive.org lending, Google Books previews. No pirated uploads.
- YouTube and streaming series count as sources (episode order shows teaching sequence).
- Never copy text word for word into the vault; summarize in our own words, quote only short phrases with attribution.

## Madhab approach (decision, 2026-10-05)
Mainstream Sunni. Short, curiosity-sparking overview of the madhabs; converts are encouraged to explore later. Practical lessons teach the common valid practice and briefly note where madhabs differ. Not a focus of the curriculum.

## Report sections
Executive summary · source table · topic × source sequencing grid · length analysis with 2–3 recommended program lengths · convert needs and gaps · where sources disagree · madhab approach · candidates for the reviewing imam's book · recommendations for T2 · questions for scholars · fact-check log · bibliography.

## Done when
- ≥30 sources, including ≥8 curricula with full topic sequences and ≥3 video series
- Every claim cited
- Fable re-checks every religious claim and ≥20% of other citations against the original; unconfirmed items marked unverified
- Disagreements and gaps listed openly
- Hub updated (status, decisions, session log)
