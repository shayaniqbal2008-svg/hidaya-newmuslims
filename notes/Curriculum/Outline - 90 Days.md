## Prayer phrase track (Days 3–30)
Day 3 gives the convert a complete minimum prayer: *Allahu akbar*; while standing, al-Fatiha's line *Al-hamdu lillahi rabbil-'alamin* and then the remembrance phrases (Abu Dawud 832); *Subhan Allah* in bowing and prostration, the shahada in the sitting, and the closing salam. From Day 8, one phrase a day replaces a placeholder with the full words.
