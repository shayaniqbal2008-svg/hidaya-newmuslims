## 3. Program architecture
- **Phase 1, Days 1–30: Foundations.** Pray from day one, get through the first weeks with family, and connect to the masjid. Ends with the honeymoon-ends lesson.
- **Phase 2, Days 31–60: Belief and worship deepened.** The six articles in depth, finish learning the prayer, the pillars, halal and haram in daily life, dress and hijab, marriage.
- **Phase 3, Days 61–90: Living it.** Character, work and money, Islamophobia, not judging others, the four schools briefly, and building your own plan after day 90.
- **Calendar modules** (fire on the date, whatever day the convert is on): **Ramadan** (from ~2 weeks before), **Eid** (both), **Thanksgiving and Christmas** (from ~1 week before, *new in the re-evaluation*: family holidays are a top pain point and no source covers them). Content for the holiday module waits on scholar Q5.
- **Welcome back flow:** after 3+ missed days, a gentle return lesson; no streak shaming.

## 7. Tone rules (from the evidence)
- Gentle, adult, warm; never scolding. No red-ink "you did it wrong."
- Never present a reduced obligation as allowed; always separate *struggling* from *denying*.
- One clear default, plus "other valid views exist; ask your imam."
- Examples and names don't default to South Asian or Arab culture (converts are ~50% Black, 17% Hispanic, 25% white).
- Privacy: no public shahada sharing, no leaderboards; the convert controls who knows.
- Welcome back after gaps, no shame.
