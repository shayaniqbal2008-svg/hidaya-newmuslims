## Glossary additions

| Term | Arabic | Plain meaning | First taught |
|---|---|---|---|
| akhirah | الآخرة | The Hereafter: the life after this one, after death and the Day of Judgment | Day 41 |
| Arafah | عرفة | The plain near Makkah where pilgrims stand in prayer on the 9th of Dhul Hijjah, the heart of Hajj; also the name of that day | Day 47 |
| Dhul Hijjah | ذو الحجة | The twelfth month of the Islamic calendar, the month of Hajj and Eid al-Adha | Day 47 |
| fidya | فدية | A payment in place of fasting by someone who can never fast (old age, lasting illness): feeding a poor person for each missed day | Day 44 |
| iftar | إفطار | The meal that breaks the fast at sunset | Day 44 |
| ihram | إحرام | The sacred state a pilgrim enters for Hajj or Umrah, with its own rules; men wear two plain white cloths | Day 47 |
| Jahannam | جهنم | Hell: the place of punishment in the Hereafter, which Allah warns about | Day 41 |
| Jannah | الجنة | Paradise: the garden of joy in the Hereafter, beyond anything we can imagine | Day 41 |
| mahr | مهر | The marriage gift a groom gives his bride; it belongs to her alone | Day 42 |
| Makkah | مكة | The city in Arabia where the Ka'bah stands; the place of Hajj and Umrah (often spelled Mecca) | Day 47 |
| nikah | نكاح | The Islamic marriage contract: both agree freely, with witnesses and a mahr | Day 42 |
| nisab | نصاب | The minimum amount of savings (measured in gold or silver) above which zakat is due after a lunar year | Day 46 |
| sa'i | سعي | Walking seven times between the hills of Safa and Marwah, part of Hajj and Umrah | Day 47 |
| sadaqah | صدقة | Voluntary charity: any amount, any time, even a kind act | Day 46 |
| suhoor | سحور | The meal eaten before dawn on a fasting day | Day 44 |
| talbiyah | تلبية | The pilgrim's call, *Labbayk Allahumma labbayk* ("Here I am, O Allah, here I am"), said throughout Hajj and Umrah | Day 47 |
| tawaf | طواف | Walking around the Ka'bah seven times in worship, part of Hajj and Umrah | Day 47 |
| tawakkul | توكل | Trusting Allah with the outcome after doing your part ("tie your camel, then trust Allah") | Day 43 |
| Umrah | عمرة | The "lesser pilgrimage" to Makkah: ihram, tawaf, sa'i and cutting the hair. It can be done any time of year | Day 47 |
| wali | ولي | A woman's marriage guardian: a trustworthy Muslim man who looks out for her and checks a suitor. The imam can help converts find one | Day 42 |

*Other batches (e.g. the Ramadan modules) may add suhoor, iftar or fidya; keep one row each.*
