## Glossary additions

| Term | Arabic | Plain meaning | First taught |

| al-Aqsa | الأقصى | "The farthest": al-Masjid al-Aqsa, the masjid in Jerusalem the Prophet ﷺ was taken to on the Night Journey | seerah-18 |
| al-Buraq | البراق | The white riding animal that carried the Prophet ﷺ to Jerusalem on the Night Journey | seerah-18 |
| al-Siddiq | الصديق | "The one who affirms the truth": the title of Abu Bakr | seerah-18 |
| 'Aqabah | العقبة | A place near Makkah where people from Madinah met the Prophet ﷺ at night and pledged to protect him | seerah-19 |
