# Seerah episodes 30–41: additions

No new verses or hadith were needed. Every reference used in `seerah-30`–`seerah-41` is already in the [[Citation Bank]] (each re-read in full with `cite.py` on 2026-10-09). The rows below only add the episodes to "Used on" (the merge keeps the existing gist and text). The glossary terms are **not used in the frontmatter `glossary:` lists yet**; the episodes gloss them inline instead. Once they're merged, add them to the lists (Ansar → 33, 34; Mother of the Believers → 31, 40; siwak → 39).

One note for the bank's maintainer: Tirmidhi 3895's English text says "the best of you is the best to his wives"; the bank's gist says "families" (the Arabic *ahl* covers both). The episodes follow the bank's gist.
