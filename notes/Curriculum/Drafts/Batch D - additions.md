## Glossary additions

| Term | Arabic | Plain meaning | First taught |
|---|---|---|---|
| 'alayhis-salam | عليه السلام | "Peace be upon him." Said after the name of any prophet other than Muhammad ﷺ, and after the angels' names | Day 39 |
| al-Hakim | الحكيم | "The Wise": a name of Allah. Nothing He does is without a reason | Day 34 |
| al-Latif | اللطيف | "The Subtle, the Gently Kind": a name of Allah, whose kindness often works quietly | Day 34 |
| ar-Razzaq | الرزاق | "The Provider": a name of Allah. Jobs and paychecks are means; He is the source | Day 34 |
| as-Salam | السلام | "Peace": a name of Allah, free of every flaw and the source of peace | Day 34 |
| ash-Shakur | الشكور | "The Appreciative": a name of Allah, who notices and rewards even small good deeds | Day 34 |
| at-Tawwab | التواب | "The One who keeps accepting repentance": a name of Allah | Day 34 |
| Ayat al-Kursi | آية الكرسي | "The Verse of the Throne" (Qur'an 2:255), about Allah's greatness and care. Many recite it after prayers and before sleep | Day 32 |
| Dawud | داود | David: a prophet and king, given the Zabur (Psalms) | Day 37 |
| fiqh | فقه | Islamic law: the detailed rulings on worship and daily life, worked out by scholars | Day 40 |
| hadith | حديث | A report of what the Prophet ﷺ said, did or approved | Day 31 |
| hafiz | حافظ | Someone who has memorized the whole Qur'an | Day 37 |
| haya' | حياء | Modesty and a sense of dignity, inside and out. The Prophet ﷺ called it part of faith | Day 38 |
| hijab | حجاب | Modest covering. Usually means a Muslim woman's head covering, but the idea of modesty applies to men and women | Day 38 |
| Iblis | إبليس | Satan: one of the jinn, who refused Allah's command. Not a fallen angel | Day 36 |
| Ibrahim | إبراهيم | Abraham: a prophet, called "a Muslim" in the Qur'an; the Salat Ibrahimiyya mentions him | Day 39 |
| jinn | جن | Unseen beings made from fire, with free will like humans | Day 36 |
| Musa | موسى | Moses: a prophet to whom Allah spoke directly, given the Tawrah (Torah) | Day 37 |
| nabi | نبي | A prophet: someone who receives revelation from Allah | Day 39 |
| Nuh | نوح | Noah: one of the earliest prophets | Day 39 |
| rasul | رسول | A messenger: a prophet also sent to deliver a message to a people | Day 39 |
| shirk | شرك | Worshipping anything besides Allah, or treating a created thing as having powers only He has. The opposite of tawhid | Day 32 |
| Tawrah | التوراة | The Torah: the scripture Allah gave to Musa (Moses) | Day 37 |
| tawhid | توحيد | Allah's oneness: one God, with no partner or equal, who alone is worshipped | Day 32 |
| Zabur | الزبور | The Psalms: the scripture Allah gave to Dawud (David) | Day 37 |
