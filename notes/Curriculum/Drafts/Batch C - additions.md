## Glossary additions

| Term | Arabic | Plain meaning | First taught |
|---|---|---|---|
| Astaghfirullah | أستغفر الله | "I ask Allah's forgiveness." Said three times right after every prayer, and any time | Day 29 |
| Hajj | الحج | The pilgrimage to Makkah, required once in a lifetime of those who are able | Day 28 |
| ihsan | إحسان | Excellence in worship: worshipping Allah as though you see Him, knowing He sees you | Day 28 |
| iman | إيمان | Faith: believing in Allah, His angels, books, messengers, the Last Day and His decree | Day 28 |
| Injil | الإنجيل | The Gospel: the scripture Muslims believe Allah gave to Jesus | Day 27 |
| Isa | عيسى | Jesus, son of Mary: a great prophet and the Messiah in Islam | Day 27 |
| Maryam | مريم | Mary, mother of Jesus. A chapter of the Qur'an is named after her | Day 27 |
| qadar | قدر | Allah's decree: nothing happens outside His knowledge and will. The sixth article of faith | Day 28 |
| Ramadan | رمضان | The month of fasting, the ninth month of the Islamic calendar | Day 28 |
| Salat Ibrahimiyya | الصلاة الإبراهيمية | The blessings on the Prophet ﷺ said in the last sitting of the prayer, after the tashahhud; it mentions Ibrahim (Abraham) | Day 26 |
| sujud al-sahw | سجود السهو | The forgetfulness prostration: two extra prostrations at the end of a prayer that repair a required part missed by mistake | Day 26 |
| tashahhud | تشهد | The words said in the sitting of the prayer, starting *at-tahiyyatu lillah* and ending with the shahada | Day 22 |
| waswasa | وسوسة | Nagging, repeated doubts (often about wudu or prayer). The cure is to go with what you're sure of | Day 26 |
| zakat | زكاة | The yearly required charity: a set share (usually 2.5%) of savings above a minimum, given to people in need | Day 28 |
