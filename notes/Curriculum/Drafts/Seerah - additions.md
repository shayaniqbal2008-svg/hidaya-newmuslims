# Seerah: Citation Bank additions

Everything below was checked with `cite.py` on 2026-10-08 (hadith: the open hadith database, sunnah.com numbering; Qur'an: Saheeh International from the quran.com API). Each gist was compared against the full text, not just the opening lines. Grades for collections outside Bukhari and Muslim are the ones the dataset gives (al-Albani and others); only sahih/hasan hadith are included. Rows for hadith already in the bank are here only to add seerah episodes to their "Used on" column (the merge keeps the existing gist). Merged with `Tools/merge_additions.py`. Context: [[Seerah Outline]].

**Deliberately not added** (see [[Seerah Fact-Check Log]]): Tirmidhi 3620 (Bahira the monk; graded munkar/da'if in the dataset); Bukhari 3883 (Abu Talib's fate; sensitive, Q42, not taught). The spider-and-dove, *Tala'a al-badru*, Ta'if du'a and "no blame on you today" reports are outside the six collections and are not cited.
