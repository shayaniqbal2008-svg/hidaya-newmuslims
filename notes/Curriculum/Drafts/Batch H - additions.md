## Glossary additions

| Term | Arabic | Plain meaning | First taught |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ashura | عاشوراء | The 10th of Muharram, the day Allah saved Musa (Moses) and his people; a recommended fast | Day 79 |
| Eid al-Adha | عيد الأضحى | "The festival of sacrifice" on the 10th of Dhul Hijjah, during Hajj, remembering Ibrahim | Day 79 |
| Eid al-Fitr | عيد الفطر | "The festival of breaking the fast" on the 1st of Shawwal, right after Ramadan | Day 79 |
| Hijrah | الهجرة | The Prophet's migration from Makkah to Madinah in 622 CE, the starting point of the Muslim calendar | Day 79 |
| Hijri | هجري | The Muslim lunar calendar, counted from the Hijrah | Day 79 |
| inna lillahi wa inna ilayhi raji'un | إنا لله وإنا إليه راجعون | "We belong to Allah, and to Him we return." Said on hearing of a death or any loss (Qur'an 2:156) | Day 77 |
| jihad | جهاد | "Striving." Mostly the struggle against your own ego and bad habits and striving to do good; in Islamic law it also covers lawful defensive war with strict rules, never attacks on civilians | Day 71 |
| Muharram | محرم | The first month of the Muslim year, one of the four sacred months | Day 79 |
| Shawwal | شوال | The tenth month of the Muslim year, which begins with Eid al-Fitr | Day 79 |
