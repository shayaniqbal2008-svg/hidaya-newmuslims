# Seerah Voice Guide (episodes 06–41)

Models: `Lessons/Seerah/seerah-01`–`05`.

## Screen pattern (13–14 screens)
`hook` → `learn` → interactive → `learn` → `timeline` → `learn` → interactive → `learn` (what this means for you) → `scenario` → `ask_ai` → (`reflect`) → `do_today` → `takeaway` → `closing`.
- **Hook:** open on a vivid moment, starting with a bold line: *"**Nobody wanted the orphan.**"*
- **Learn:** a bold 2–5 word title, then about 30–40 words of story and at most 2 bubbles. Total ≈200 words.
- **Closing:** a one-line teaser: *"Next episode: …"*

## Interactive mix
Always include a `timeline` (the episode's events, with `when` as an age or "about" a year), a convert-life `scenario` and an `ask_ai`. Add two more, and vary them: `fill` for a key verse, `order` for a sequence, `sort`, `reveal` for people or perspectives, `choice` that asks *why*, or a "you're there, what do you do?" scenario. The best answer's feedback says what really happened.

## Tone
1. **Story first; the moral comes once, later.** *"The nurses looked at a fatherless baby and saw a bad deal. Allah saw His last messenger."*
2. **Speak to a lonely adult, never down to them.** No guilt, no "should". *"If becoming Muslim has cost you closeness with your family, you aren't forgotten."*
3. **Be honest about the sources, out loud.** *"The hadith gives no age, and other reports differ on the details. We tell it the simple way the hadith does."*

## Cautious wording
- Hadith: paraphrase, add the ref, never use quotation marks. Qur'an: quote it in italics, `[Q x:y, Saheeh Intl.]`.
- Seerah-only facts get a short inline tag, `[IslamQA 11575; Qalam ep. 12; Dar al-Ifta 168]`, with the full names in Sources.
- Facts the log marks *cautious*: "according to the early biographers", "X later said", "is reported", "about".
- Disputed dates: say only what the outline allows, plus the inline `[SCHOLAR: Qn]`.
- Weak stories stay off screens. In the AI guide's *Likely questions* they become "a well-known story whose chain scholars consider weak" (Q43).
- Every new factual sentence needs a Fact-Check Log row.

## Linking to the convert's life
Turn the outline's *Lesson today* into one screen naming a real situation: family pulling away, being overlooked at the masjid, a boss wanting a lie, "your old life was all wrong". Then write a scenario using the Spec §5 cast. Non-best options say "Not quite. …" and teach. Respect the family's faith, and never compare it to Makkan idolatry.

## Sensitive topics
They are never on screens. In the AI guide context, give the facts with refs and the rules "don't deny the texts / don't speculate / offer the imam", with the Q flag inline. Don't write chips that invite the topic.

## Spelling
Muhammad ﷺ (once per screen) · Allah · Ibrahim (Abraham), Isma'il (Ishmael): gloss the English name on first use · Jibril · Ka'bah · Makkah, Madinah · Quraysh, Banu Hashim · 'Abdullah, Aminah, 'Abd al-Muttalib, Abu Talib · Halimah (Banu Sa'd), Thuwaybah, Umm Ayman (Barakah) · Khadijah · Abu Bakr, 'Umar, 'Ali, Hamzah · al-Amin · *Hilf al-Fudul* · hijra · "about 570 CE". Use straight apostrophes for 'ayn.

## Do-today
`text` is a one-line mission ("Be someone's Halimah today.") with 2–3 tiny `steps` they can do within 24 hours. Always `log: null`.

## Pictures and frontmatter
Scenes and Lucide icons only; never a prophet, angel or Companion. `skills: [T2, B6]`, plus B4 for family episodes or T3 for hard-question ones. `minutes: 6`. `glossary` takes Glossary.md terms only. Gloss other terms inline and request them in your additions file.
