## Glossary additions

| Term | Arabic | Plain meaning | First taught |
|---|---|---|---|
| tarawih | تراويح | The extra night prayer of Ramadan, prayed in pairs of rak'ahs after Isha, usually at the masjid | ramadan-4 |
| Laylat al-Qadr | ليلة القدر | The Night of Decree: the night the Qur'an was first revealed, better than a thousand months, sought in the odd nights of Ramadan's last ten | ramadan-5 |
| i'tikaf | اعتكاف | Staying in the masjid for a set time to devote yourself to worship, especially in the last ten days of Ramadan | ramadan-5 |
| Zakat al-Fitr | زكاة الفطر | The small set charity given for each person before the Eid al-Fitr prayer, so the poor can celebrate too | ramadan-6 |
| udhiyah | أضحية | The animal sacrificed for Allah on Eid al-Adha or the days after it (also called qurbani) | hajj-1 |
| Takbir al-Tashriq | تكبير التشريق | The takbir said after each obligatory prayer from Fajr on the 9th of Dhul Hijjah to Asr on the 13th (necessary in the Hanafi school) | hajj-2 |
| Isma'il | إسماعيل | Ishmael: a prophet, son of Ibrahim and Hajar, who helped his father raise the Ka'bah | eid-adha |
| Hajar | هاجر | Hagar: Isma'il's mother, whose search for water between two hills pilgrims retrace in the sa'i | eid-adha |
