# First Instructions on Worship (Hanafi edition)
**By:** Samah Marei (Hanafi edition), ZHIC New Muslim Curriculum, 1st ed. 2010. 39 pages, full text readable. Free from the publisher's distributor. Companion to [[Source - The Revealed Path]].

**Structure:** Part I Preparing for prayer (water; cleaning after the toilet; wudu step by step and what breaks it; ghusl; menstruation) · Part II Prayer (times, names and rak'ahs; praying on time and missing it; the call to prayer; **how to pray 2, 3 and 4 rak'ahs**; praying in a group; witr, Friday, Eid, tarawih, traveler's prayer) · Part III Fasting (who fasts, validity, missed fasts) · Appendix: transliterated recitations and supplications with audio.

**Value: high, for one job.** It's a clean, step-by-step Hanafi prayer and purification manual written for converts, from the same curriculum the reviewing imam may have meant. It's the best reference we have for the Day 3–5 wudu and prayer lessons if the pilot masjid'sets Hanafi as its default. It has a full transliteration appendix, which fits the "learn the phrases one at a time" design.

**Gaps:** no guidance for the period before Fatiha is memorized (use the dhikr substitute from [[Research Notes - Core Essentials and Madhab Map]], pending the reviewing imam). No convert pain points (by design; it's a worship manual).
