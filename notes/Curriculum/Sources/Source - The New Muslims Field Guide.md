# The New Muslim's Field Guide (Theresa Corbin & Kaighla Um Dayo, 2018)
153 pages, ~57,000 words, 18 chapters. Written by two American converts. Casual, funny, blunt tone.

**Value: gold for pain points.** It's the book closest to our positioning, so it's both our best content reference and the book we need to differentiate from (see [[Positioning - Pain-Point Curriculum]]).

**Chapters:** 1 You're Still You · 2 Culture & Islam · 3 What's in a Name? · 4 All in the (Non-Muslim) Family · 5 Six Pillars of Faith · 6 Five Pillars of Islam · 7 Run to Prayer · 8 Know Thy Sources · 9 Slow and Steady · 10 What the Hijab?! (men and women) · 11 The Masjid: What to Know Before You Go · 12 The Company You Keep · 13 Sex, Marriage & Love · 14–15 The Marriage Question for Sisters / Brothers · 16 Dealing with Islamophobia · 17 Islam Should Make Your Heart Soft · 18 When in the Field

**What it adds**
- **Identity first:** Islam enriches who you are; it doesn't erase you. Culture has a real place (*'urf*): an American-style wedding is fine if nothing in it is forbidden. Supports a Day 1–2 "you're still you" message.
- **A letter to your family** (ch. 4) that converts can hand to relatives. **Feature idea for the app:** a shareable "letter to my family" the convert can personalize and send (written in our own words).
- **Burnout from over-zeal** (ch. 9): converts try to do everything on day one (all sunnah prayers, niqab or a big beard, Monday–Thursday fasts, memorizing the whole Qur'an) and burn out. Cites "the religion is ease" (Bukhari) and "the best deeds are regular even if few."
- **First masjid visit guide** (ch. 11): shoes, sitting on the floor, where to go. Maps directly onto our Day 7 lesson.
- **Keeping non-Muslim friends** (ch. 12): be kind to everyone; step back only from friends who push you toward what you're leaving. Also gives its own reading of Qur'an 5:51 (*awliya* as "protectors," not "friends"). That's an interpretive claim, so a scholar should check it before we use it.
- **Don't become judgmental** (ch. 17): new Muslims who "get it" can start judging others. A good Phase 3 lesson.
- **The few-weeks-in slump** (ch. 18): "Did I make the right decision?" and friends dropping away. This is the honeymoon-ends moment from your Reddit notes; supports the day 21–30 lesson.

**Gaps (full-text check):** praying in a non-Muslim home (0 mentions), Christmas or family holidays (0), iman going up and down (1 passing mention). Marriage gets three chapters, which is more than we need early.

**How we're different:** it's a book read once, with no sequence by day, no prayer instruction step by step, and no masjid or mentor layer. Ours delivers the same kind of help at the moment it's needed, and tells the masjid who's struggling.
