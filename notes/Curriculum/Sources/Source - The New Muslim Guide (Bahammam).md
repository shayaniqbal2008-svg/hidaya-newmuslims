# The New Muslim Guide (Fahd Salem Bahammam)

**File:** ![[The-New-Muslim-Guide-English.pdf]] (in `Attachments/`)
**By:** Fahd Salem Bahammam; Contemporary Guide Company (Saudi Arabia). 2017 print edition, 260 pages, ISBN 978-603-00-9654-1.
**Already in T1?** Yes. Full 13-section order taken from newmuslimguide.com in [[Research Notes - Books and Guides]]; ranked #1 candidate for the reviewing imam's book in [[Research Report]] §9.

## Can it be read?
**Not directly.** The PDF is a scan of the printed pages with no text layer (260 pages produce 29 words). Reading it would need OCR software installed on your Mac. **Not needed:** the same content is published free as text at [newmuslimguide.com](https://www.newmuslimguide.com/en) in 26+ languages, which is what T1 used and what T2 will use for lesson-level detail.

## Structure (from the website, confirmed in T1)
Preliminaries (13 short topics: purpose of life, no intermediary with God, how to find a ruling, moderation, judge Islam by its teachings not by Muslims) → Your Faith → Your Purification → Your Prayer → Your Fast → Your Zakat → Your Pilgrimage → Your Financial Transactions → Your Food & Drink → Your Dress Code → Your Family → Your Moral Character → Your New Life.

## Verdict
No new structural information. Useful in T2 as a cross-check for practical rulings (purification and prayer detail), via the website rather than the scan. Salafi-leaning; check fiqh points against the madhab map in [[Research Notes - Core Essentials and Madhab Map]].
