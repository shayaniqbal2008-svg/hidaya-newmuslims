# Being Muslim: A Practical Guide (Asad Tarsin, 2015; foreword by Hamza Yusuf)
271 pages, ~92,000 words. Traditional Sunni. The shared textbook for Ta'leef, SeekersGuidance and IFN's convert classes (see [[Research Notes - Programs and Courses]]).

**Structure:** Starting Point (the beginning; Islam in context; learning Islam) → Belief (shahada; God; angels; scriptures; messengers; Judgment Day; divine decree) → Worship (legal rulings; prayer; zakat; fasting; Hajj; other worship; rulings particular to women) → Spiritual Refinement (consciousness; the path; destructive vices; saving virtues; noble character) → The Prophet → The Qur'an → Holy Days → Lifestyle.

**Value: moderate.** Its order was already in T1. Best used as a **reference for writing Phase 2–3 lessons** on belief, worship detail, spiritual refinement and character; careful, well-sourced and scholar-endorsed, which helps with scholar review. "Rulings Particular to Women" is useful for women-specific lessons.

**Gaps:** few convert pain points (non-Muslim family mentioned 7 times, iman dips 0, praying at home 0). Overlaps heavily with [[Source - The Revealed Path]].
