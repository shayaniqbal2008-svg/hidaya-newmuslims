# The Revealed Path: A Guide Book for New Muslims

**File:** ![[English-E-Book-1-Guide-Book-for-New-Muslims.pdf]] (in `Attachments/`)
**By:** Hasan Lam (Parts I–III), Ramzy Ajem (Part IV). Zayed House for Islamic Culture (UAE), produced by Razi Group (Canada) under Tabah Foundation. 2nd ed. 2014. 133 pages, ~36,000 words, full text readable.
**Already in T1?** Yes. Profiled from its contents page in [[Research Notes - Books and Guides]] and ranked #2 candidate for the reviewing imam's book in [[Research Report]] §9.
**Copyright:** all rights reserved (fair-use excerpts only). We summarize and cite; lessons are never copied.

## Structure (confirmed from the full text)
Introduction (travel-guide metaphor; advice: intention, small consistent deeds, rely on God; the Hadith of Gabriel as the frame)
- **Part I, Islam:** 1 Overview (essence, five pillars, importance of knowledge, the revealed law and the four madhhabs) · 2 Shahada (first steps, a new beginning) · 3 Prayer (connection to God; bringing prayer to life; prayer at other times) · 4 Zakat · 5 Fasting · 6 Hajj
- **Part II, Iman:** 7 Tree of faith · 8 God (names, attributes, "living with the Merciful") · 9 Angels · 10 Books (the Qur'an in our lives) · 11 Messengers (sira, loving the Prophet) · 12 Last Day · 13 Destiny
- **Part III, Ihsan:** 14 Overview · 15 Heart and limbs · 16 Repentance, fear and hope, patience · 17 Company and surroundings · 18 Dhikr
- **Part IV, Rights and responsibilities:** 19 Rights of God and the Messenger · 20 The self (moderation, food, harmful substances, clothing and modesty) · 21 Family (mainly marriage and children) · 22 Community of believers · 23 Living in non-Muslim societies, other faiths · 24 Financial interactions (work, employers) · 25 (closing)

## What it adds beyond T1
1. **Best single reference for writing belief and ihsan lessons (Phase 2–3).** Chapters 8, 12, 13 and 16 explain God's attributes, the afterlife, destiny, repentance and hope at exactly a new Muslim's reading level. Good model for tone: warm, scholarly, never scolding.
2. **Explicit gradualism rationale** quoting Ibn Hajar: learning should be gradual and start easy so it's received with delight; learn what you need for current obligations (how to pray) first. Supports our design and gives a citable traditional source for it.
3. **"Living in non-Muslim societies"** (ch. 23): don't isolate; the Prophet took part in Meccan civic life. Useful for a Phase 3 lesson.
4. **"Small consistent deeds"** hadith used as study advice. Fits the 5-minute daily format and the welcome-back tone.

## What it does NOT cover (checked by full-text search)
Telling or living with **non-Muslim parents** (0 mentions), **praying in a non-Muslim household** (0), **hijab** and how people react (0), **iman going up and down** (0), **name change** (0), **Christmas or family holidays** (0), **loneliness** (only "avoid isolation" in society). Family = marriage and children, not the convert's birth family. So it misses almost every pain point in [[Personal Research via reddit on pain points for converts]].

## Verdict
Useful as a **content reference for lesson writing**, not a structural change. Its order is already in the T1 sequencing grid. Its companion, *First Instructions on Worship* (separate manuals for each madhab), would be more valuable for the prayer lessons.
