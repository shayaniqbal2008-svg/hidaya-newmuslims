# Welcome to Islam: A Step-by-Step Guide for New Muslims (Mustafa Umar, 2nd ed. 2011)
32 pages, ~6,000 words. Written for the person who just took shahada and is asking "OK, now what?"

**Value: gold for structure.** It's the only source we have that is built as **days**: about 10 minutes of reading each, one section a day.

| Day | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | What the shahada commits you to; three core beliefs (one God, revelation, afterlife); take a shower to mark a fresh start; **telling friends and family** |
| 2 | Your name (no need to change it unless the meaning is bad); wudu step by step; prayer times; finding the qibla; "five times a day" |
| 3 | Prayer movements and the first Arabic phrases; at the masjid, just follow the imam and learn the rest over time |
| 4 | Dress code; diet (pork, alcohol and drugs, dead animals); gambling and interest |
| 5 | Friday prayer; the sources of Islam; **common challenges**: information overload, discipline, staying part of the community, persecution (contact CAIR), and conflicting voices (consult a qualified scholar; differences on minor issues are normal) |

**Ideas to borrow**
- Every day ends with a "What's next" note repeating *slow and steady: don't overload, don't be lazy, keep a balance.* That's a ready-made closing line for our lessons.
- **Family advice is practical, not preachy:** sound out family before telling them (e.g., "Hey Dad, I've been reading about Islam, what do you think?"), and if they're hostile, keep it private for a few months, especially if you aren't financially independent. Goes straight into our Day 6 family lesson (in our own words).
- Ending with a "common challenges" heads-up before they happen matches our honeymoon-ends lesson.

**⚠️ Conflict to flag for scholars:** on Day 2 it tells converts that until they learn the prayer, they can sit during each prayer time and thank Allah, and "this will be your prayer." Tier-1 sources say all five prayers are due from day one with the bare minimum of actions (SeekersGuidance 167692; IslamWeb 529861). Our Day 4 lesson should follow the Tier-1 view; added to the scholar questions.

**Gaps:** iman dips, hijab reactions, praying in a non-Muslim home, holidays, loneliness (only "don't isolate yourself").
