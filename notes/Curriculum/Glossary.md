# Glossary

> [!info] How this is used
> Every Arabic or Islamic term a lesson uses is defined here, in plain English. The app shows these as tap-for-meaning popups. Lessons list their terms in the frontmatter (`glossary:`); the checker confirms each one exists here. Add new terms in alphabetical order. Definitions are short and are written for someone in their first week.
