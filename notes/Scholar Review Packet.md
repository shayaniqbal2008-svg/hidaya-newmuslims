# Hidaya: questions for scholars

*Shareable web version (private until you share it from its Share menu): https://claude.ai/artifact/HSWj7qBafYgdvLqrLu5hwR*

*Google Doc version (2026-10-07; share with "Anyone with the link → Commenter" so scholars can comment): https://docs.google.com/document/d/1cWcA4y7D_pMmIg9N9sUxin-FK-1ILiVPEq0SyCxG0R8/edit*

*A general packet for any scholar, sheikh or imam reviewing Hidaya. Source of truth: [[Curriculum Spec]] §9 (question numbers match). Imam-specific asks for the the pilot masjid pilot (the masjid's default school, the book's name) are kept separately in the hub.*

---

**Assalamu alaykum, and thank you for taking a look.**

**What Hidaya is.** A phone app that walks a new Muslim through their first 90 days, five minutes a day: short interactive lessons (prayer, belief, family, food, fasting, community) and an AI guide they can ask questions, which hands anything personal or sensitive to their imam. Masjids give it to their new Muslims. Each masjid sets the school of fiqh (madhab) its converts are taught, and every lesson follows that school. The curriculum is mainstream Sunni, written in plain English for people with no background, and built around the problems converts actually face (family, praying at work, loneliness, doubts, faith dipping).

**Try it (5 minutes):** open **https://your-app.vercel.app/demo**, pick men's or women's guidance and a school, and every lesson is unlocked. No sign-up or email needed.

**How we handle open questions.** Where a lesson depends on a question scholars may answer differently, we've written the most cautious mainstream position we could find and flagged it for review. Those 45 questions are below (Q37–Q45, in section I, cover the optional seerah track), each with what the app teaches today.

**How to reply.** For each question you want to answer, reply with its number and one of:
- **Agree**: the current wording is fine.
- **Change**: what it should say, and your reasoning or source if you can.
- **Depends**: how it differs by school or situation, so we can show the right version to the right masjid.

You don't need to answer everything; even five answers help. Please say which school you follow and, if you're happy to, how we may credit you.

## A. Prayer

| # | Question | What Hidaya teaches now |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | How should an app that serves masjids of different schools handle the method of prayer? | Each masjid sets its school; lessons show that school's method, and a masjid with no school set gets common ground with "your masjid will show you". A new Muslim is told to follow their masjid while learning. |
| Q2 | Before a new Muslim has memorized al-Fatiha, what should they say while standing? Is a card or phone allowed during prayer? | From Day 3: the first verse of al-Fatiha (*al-hamdu lillahi rabbil-'alamin*), then the remembrance phrases taught to a man who couldn't yet memorize Qur'an [Abu Dawud 832]; all of al-Fatiha by Day 16. No card or phone during prayer. *Does this one verse meet the minimum for a learner, especially in the Hanafi school?* |
| Q4 | How should we phrase "you're still learning" for prayer? | "All five prayers are yours from today. Pray each one with what you know so far." |
| Q11 | Some guides tell a brand-new Muslim to "sit and thank Allah" instead of praying until they learn. Should they instead pray all five with the minimum from day one? | Pray all five with the minimum from day one. |
| Q13 | May a new Muslim recite a translation of al-Fatiha in prayer? | Never offered; the translation is for understanding only. |
| Q15 | Someone takes their shahada during Asr time. Do they owe that day's Dhuhr? | "Start with the next prayer that's due"; ask your imam about the day you converted. |
| Q17 | When does Isha end? | "Until dawn; try to pray before midnight." |
| Q18 | Witr: required or recommended, 3 rak'ahs or 1, and when should a new Muslim learn it? | Hanafi masjids: required, 3 rak'ahs after Isha, taught simply on Day 8 with a short du'a until the full qunut is learned (Day 57). Other masjids: a strongly recommended prayer. *Hanafi scholars: please supply or verify the Arabic of the qunut al-witr for Day 57.* |
| Q25 | A new Muslim doubts how many rak'ahs they've prayed. Hanafi: does a convert's "first time" count from their conversion? | Hanafi masjids: "If this kind of doubt is new for you, end the prayer and pray it again"; those who doubt often go with their strongest impression. |
| Q28 | How should the emphasized sunnah prayers be presented to a new Muslim? | "Not required like the five, but the Prophet ﷺ kept them: add one, keep it, then add another." The app neither calls them skippable nor dwells on blame. |

## B. Purity

| # | Question | What Hidaya teaches now |
|---|---|---|
| Q3 | Is a ghusl on becoming Muslim obligatory or recommended? | "Take your ghusl once now, as a new Muslim, before your first prayer" (it's required for anyone who has had sex, a wet dream or a period since their last full wash, which covers almost every adult). Never presented as a condition of the shahada itself. |
| Q7 | Which things break wudu, especially touching and bleeding? | Masjids with no school set: only the points all schools agree on. Hanafi masjids: also flowing blood or pus, a mouthful of vomit, and laughing aloud in prayer; touching doesn't break it. |
| Q12 | Wiping over ordinary (non-leather) socks? | Not taught in the first week; Day 58 gives the masjid's position. |
| Q26 | Practising Qur'an phrases while on a period or needing a ghusl (Hanafi: no recitation in that state). Is saying the words one at a time to learn them allowed? | Learn by listening and saying the words in your heart until clean; du'a and dhikr aloud are fine. |

## C. Fasting, zakat and food

| # | Question | What Hidaya teaches now |
|---|---|---|
| Q6 | Ordinary supermarket meat (from Christians or Jews)? | Default to zabiha or halal-certified meat; say scholars differ on ordinary meat; family dinner: eat the sides and ask your imam. |
| Q14 | Someone becomes Muslim midway through a day in Ramadan. What about the rest of that day? | Refrain for the rest of the day if you can; don't make up the earlier days; ask your imam. |
| Q16 | Zakat: does the first year start at conversion? | Nothing is owed for the years before Islam; ask your imam how to count the first year. |
| Q34 | Trace alcohol in food (e.g. vanilla extract), and gelatin from non-pork animals not slaughtered Islamically. | Avoid pork gelatin, lard and food cooked with alcohol; name trace alcohol and non-pork gelatin as differences of opinion; ask your imam. |
| Q35 | Medicine while fasting: ear drops, nasal sprays, inhalers, injections. | Nose drops break the fast (Hanafi); ear drops: scholars differ; anything else: ask your imam; never risk your health. |

## D. Family, marriage and work

| # | Question | What Hidaya teaches now |
|---|---|---|
| Q5 | Christmas, Thanksgiving and other holidays with non-Muslim family. | Keep family ties and go to the meal; skip the religious rites; greetings are left as a difference of opinion; ask your imam. |
| Q9 | Shaking hands with the opposite gender at work. | Teach the hand-on-heart greeting; note that scholars differ; ask your imam. |
| Q19 | Qur'an 5:51 and friendship with non-Muslims. | Not quoted; we teach Qur'an 60:8 (kindness and justice to those who don't fight you). |
| Q21 | A new Muslim who is already married to a non-Muslim spouse. | No ruling given in the app, for husbands or wives: be kind at home and "talk to your imam privately, soon"; don't decide alone or from videos. The AI guide refers these to the imam. |
| Q23 | Praying for a non-Muslim relative who has died. | State the Qur'an's guidance gently [Q 9:113], and what *is* allowed (grieving, attending the funeral, condolences, kindness); ask your imam. |
| Q24 | Existing interest-bearing debt (student loans, car loans, a mortgage). | Riba is serious; avoid new interest loans where possible; for existing debt, "talk to your imam about your situation". |
| Q30 | A non-Muslim parent asks for help with their own worship (e.g. a ride to church). | Keep helping in all of daily life; don't take part in the worship itself; the "getting them there" question goes to the imam. |
| Q31 | A new Muslim already working in a job whose main work is haram (e.g. serving alcohol). | Not a job to stay in long-term; talk to your imam privately, soon; start looking now; don't decide in a panic. |

## E. Modesty and appearance

| # | Question | What Hidaya teaches now |
|---|---|---|
| Q8 | Hijab: when and how to present it, and face covering. | Women see the ruling stated plainly (mainstream scholars of all four schools hold it's required past puberty) with a gentle, step-by-step path and a female mentor. Men see one general line about it, and are told their part is their own modesty, not policing sisters. Face and hands may show (feet too, Hanafi). |
| Q29 | The beard: how firmly to state it, and its length. | "Many scholars hold that keeping a beard is required; your imam can explain" [Bukhari 5892]; it can grow in steps; pushback from work or family goes to the imam. |
| Q36 | Women attending the Eid prayer. | Not required of women, but **highly encouraged**: the Prophet ﷺ had women and girls, even those on their period, come out for both Eids [Muslim 890]; on their period they join the takbir and du'a but not the prayer itself. *Later Hanafi scholars generally discouraged women attending: is this wording acceptable, and how should a Hanafi masjid present it?* |

## H. Specific wording we'd like a second look at

These came up while making the lessons interactive. None changes a ruling, but each is worth a scholar's eye.
1. **Hanafi period length** (Day 17): "a period lasts 3 to 10 days; otherwise ask…" is compressed.
2. ***Rabbighfirli*** between the two prostrations (Day 21) is shown inside the obligatory prayer.
3. **Joining the congregation late** (Day 22): "make up the missed rak'ahs with al-Fatiha and a surah" is simplified (the full detail is with the AI guide).
4. **Examples of what a woman covers** (Day 38): "your arms and legs" as examples of "the body".
5. **The four schools on screen** (Day 40): the four schools are named, but no screen says which school does what.
6. **What each of Islam's limits protects** (Day 49): only "intoxicants protect the mind" was in the original; the other pairings (family, others' rights, money) were added.
7. **Eid prayer, Hanafi** (Eid modules): the hands are folded after the third extra takbir in the first rak'ah.
8. **Dhikr counts** used for practice (e.g. "say it 10 times") are labelled as practice, not as sunnah numbers.
9. **Moon sighting**: the app says Islamic months begin with the new crescent and "your masjid announces the exact day", to cover both sighting and calculation.

## I. Seerah (an optional track on the Prophet's ﷺ life)

An optional set of about 40 short episodes on the Prophet's ﷺ life, from Arabia before Islam to his passing ([[Seerah Outline]]). Every fact is sourced to the Qur'an, a sahih or hasan hadith, or two present-day scholarly sources, and logged in [[Seerah Fact-Check Log]]. These are the sensitive points.

| # | Question | What Hidaya teaches now |
|---|---|---|
| Q37 | Aisha's age at marriage (for an optional track on the Prophet's ﷺ life) | Not on any screen. The AI guide, if asked: the sahih reports in Bukhari and Muslim give six at the contract and nine when the marriage began [Bukhari 5133]; she became one of Islam's greatest scholars; norms of that time differed and his enemies never objected; some Muslim researchers argue for an older age, others defend the narration; never deny the hadith or mock the question; offer the imam. *Is this approach and wording acceptable?* |
| Q38 | Banu Qurayzah: whether and how to mention it in the seerah track | One calm line in the Trench episode: a tribe that broke its treaty during the siege was judged afterwards by an arbiter they chose; "one of the hardest events of his life; your imam can walk you through it." The AI guide has the facts (treason in wartime, Sa'd ibn Mu'adh's ruling, boys spared, some granted amnesty), no casualty number, and never connects it to Jews today. *Should it be on screen at all, or AI guide only?* |
| Q39 | Telling the battles (Badr, Uhud, the Trench, Khaybar, Makkah, Hunayn, Tabuk) to new Muslims | As history: why each happened, his conduct, what the Qur'an said; no graphic detail, no glorifying killing; each carries his rules of war (no women or children [Bukhari 3015]); no rulings on fighting today (Day 71 frame). *Is this framing sound?* |
| Q40 | Slavery and captives in the seerah (Bilal, Zayd, Umm Ayman, Safiyyah, Mariyah, "those your right hands possess") | Say plainly that slavery existed everywhere then; highlight freeing (Bilal, Zayd, Umm Ayman, Safiyyah, the Hawazin families returned [Bukhari 2307]); don't claim Islam abolished it or that it is acceptable today; "those your right hands possess" shown as "those in your care" with the literal meaning given by the AI guide if asked; detailed questions go to the imam. *Acceptable?* |
| Q41 | His marriages (number of wives, polygyny, Zaynab bint Jahsh, Safiyyah) | Names without commentary on screen; one line: monogamous with Khadijah for about 25 years, later marriages mostly to widows or divorcees, many tied to alliances and care; "why so many" goes to the AI guide and imam. *Acceptable?* |
| Q42 | The fate of his parents and of Abu Talib | Never on screen. Sahih texts exist [Muslim 976; Bukhari 1360]; the AI guide doesn't deny them, doesn't speculate about any individual, stresses that Allah is the Judge, and offers the imam (see Q23). *How should an app speak to converts with non-Muslim parents about this?* |
| Q43 | Famous stories with weak or disputed chains (spider and dove at the cave, *Tala'a al-badru*, the du'a of Ta'if, "No blame on you today", Bahira the monk) | Left off screens. If asked: "a well-known story whose chain scholars consider weak" (or "differ on" for the spider). *Should well-loved stories like these be told with that label, or not at all?* |
| Q44 | Dates of his birth (Mawlid) and the Night Journey (27 Rajab) | Give only "Rabi' al-Awwal, by most accounts" and "about a year before the hijra"; don't discuss whether to celebrate; the AI guide sends that to the imam. *Acceptable?* |
| Q45 | The farewell sermon's line about disciplining wives (Muslim 1218) | Not quoted on screen; the episode quotes "fear Allah concerning women" and their rights; if asked, the AI guide points to Q 4:19 (live with them in kindness), his own conduct (he never struck a woman [Muslim 2328]) and the imam. *Is this selective quotation acceptable, or should the line be addressed?* |
