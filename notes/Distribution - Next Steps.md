## Market direction (2026-10-07)
**Working direction, to be checked in outreach:** converts get Hidaya **free** (App Store later; **no ads**, since ads clash with discreet mode and privacy; donations OK). **Organizations pay**, and a masjid counts as an org with one chapter. **Governments later**, once there are org deals and users.

Why, briefly:
- **Consumer with ads:** too few users for ad money (~31,000 US conversions a year) and it breaks the privacy promise. Keep consumers as the **funnel**: content plus "ask your masjid/org to get Hidaya" and a waitlist that becomes the sales pipeline. A free app still costs money per user (the AI guide), so it needs a daily cap and a payer behind it.
- **Masjids only:** reachable, but ~11 converts per masjid per year on average, so each sale is small. Still where the convert shows up, so chapter features stay.
- **Organizations:** a small pool, but they already spend on converts. Some are also competitors with their own curricula (New Muslim Academy, Ta'leef), so pitch a **partnership or white-label**: their curriculum and community, our app and AI guide.
- **Governments:** biggest prize, 12–24-month sales cycles, would want Arabic and theological control. Possible bridge: Gulf "call and guidance" offices that already run convert programs (**not researched yet**).

### Questions for Shayan's mentor and sheikh outreach
Ask before anything is sent (hub rule). Useful to ask each person:
1. After using the demo, would you recommend it to a new Muslim? What's missing or wrong?
2. Who should pay: the masjid, a national org, donors, or nobody? Would your masjid or org pay, and roughly how much a year?
3. Which orgs or people decide on new-Muslim programs, and would you introduce us?
4. Would a free app on the App Store, used without a masjid, worry you? What would it need (scholar sign-off, a default madhab, a way to reach a real imam)?
5. Where do new Muslims actually look for help in their first weeks (YouTube, Reddit, the masjid, a friend)? That's where marketing goes.
6. Would you put your name on it as a reviewing scholar?

Write the answers into each person's note in `Outreach/`, then bring them to the T5 session.
